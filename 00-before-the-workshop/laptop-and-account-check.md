# Laptop and account check

Purpose: in 10 minutes, find out what you will work with in the workshop — device, storage, mail, AI plan, desktop app, WhatsApp — so the set-up on the day fits *your* situation. The workshop runs in the desktop app of ChatGPT or Claude, in one folder on your laptop.

Tick one answer per line. There are no wrong answers; "not yet" is a perfectly good start.

## The check

| # | Question | Your answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Device** — what will you bring? | ☐ Windows laptop ☐ Mac ☐ Chromebook *(browser path)* ☐ only an iPad/tablet *(browser path — pair up or borrow a laptop)* |
| 2 | **Cloud storage** — where does chapter work live today? | ☐ Google Drive, chapter account ☐ Google Drive, private ☐ OneDrive, chapter account ☐ OneDrive, private ☐ other: ____ ☐ spread over several places |
| 3 | **Email** — which mail do you use for chapter work? | ☐ Gmail ☐ Outlook / Microsoft 365 ☐ other: ____ |
| 4 | **Role address** — is there an address that belongs to the role, e.g. `manager@<chapter-domain>`? | ☐ yes ☐ no ☐ not sure |
| 4b | **Chapter accounts** — does the chapter have accounts in Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, at least one for you (`manager@`) and one for your Chair (`chair@`)? | ☐ yes, both ☐ only mine ☐ no ☐ not sure |
| 5 | **Paid AI plan** — which one do you have? | ☐ Claude (Pro or higher) ☐ ChatGPT (Plus or higher) ☐ Gemini (paid) ☐ Copilot (paid or work account) ☐ not yet — I set it up this week |

| 8 | **One wish** — the task you would most like AI to take off your desk (1–2 sentences) | ______________________ |
| 9 | **The third time** — which question do you answer every year, for the third time, when a new officer starts? | ______________________ |
| 10 | **Gone again** *(if you have been in the role a while)* — one tool that disappeared again in your chapter: what was the reason? | ______________________ |

Your trainer may send a short form for these answers, together with your self-test result. That is how the trainer
knows before the day where everyone stands — plan, laptop, self-test — so nobody is asked about it in the room.
Question 9 feeds your first lab: the answer goes into your chapter facts, so you do not have to write it a fourth time.
No form? Just bring your answers.

## What your answers mean for the day

| If you ticked … | Then in the workshop … |
|---|---|
| Only an iPad/tablet, or a Chromebook | You take the browser path with a neighbour and set up the desktop app on a laptop later — everything is in the kit |
| No desktop app possible (IT-managed laptop, or Work switched off in your workspace) | The browser path: the same labs, and you save the files yourself. The desktop app follows on a laptop you control |
| No synced drive on the laptop | Install its sync app before the day (OneDrive comes with Windows; Mac: the OneDrive app or *Google Drive for desktop*). Not possible? A local folder today, moved into the drive later |
| Storage "private" or "several places" | You build the folder structure today wherever you can; moving it into a chapter account later is one step ([../01-foundations/where-your-data-lives.md](../01-foundations/where-your-data-lives.md)) |
| No role address, or no chapter accounts for you and your Chair | Common — and the one thing your Chair can unlock. Chapter accounts in Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace for at least you and your Chair are the kit's second prerequisite: they make the handover work and let your Chair read the chapter folder. Ask your Chair now — the request is ready in [../05-officer-handover/role-accounts.md](../05-officer-handover/role-accounts.md). On the day you build in your own drive and move the folder across once the accounts exist |
| No paid AI plan yet | A paid plan is the first prerequisite. Set it up before the workshop (about 20 minutes, see [../01-foundations/paid-plan-and-training-off.md](../01-foundations/paid-plan-and-training-off.md); before anyone pays, the plan pick and check in [../01-foundations/business-plan-checklist.md](../01-foundations/business-plan-checklist.md)). If it is not ready on the day, watch with your neighbour and set it up the same evening. |
| WhatsApp only on the phone | Fine — the WhatsApp step works with an export from your phone |

## Common worries — short answers

| Worry | Answer |
|---|---|
| "I have no time for this." | That's why the workshop ends with something running, not with homework. After that, the bet: the hours come from searching and answering twice. Test it with your own week — your log will tell you. |
| "My chapter doesn't pay for an AI plan yet." | The one-pager in the kit is written for exactly that ExCo decision. Until then, set up a paid plan before the workshop — it is the first prerequisite. |
| "What about member data?" | Paid plan with training switched off, one chapter storage, WhatsApp read on your own laptop, and you send every message yourself. |
| "I'm not technical." | If you use Canva and Zoom, you can do this. In the desktop app the assistant saves the files for you. No code until Level 3 — and Level 3 is optional. |
| "Can I use my iPhone?" | Yes, for ordinary chats and drafts on the go — same app, same account. The folder task stays on the laptop, where the folder lives. |
| "Is my laptop safe if the AI works in a folder?" | You choose the folder: allow access to `CM-Workspace` only, and read what the app asks for. You still send every message yourself. |
| "My board will never do this." | The rules for working with your CM aren't an AI question — the chair sets them; AI just makes them easy to keep. The kit has a page for that ([../06-chapter-rules/operating-guardrails.md](../06-chapter-rules/operating-guardrails.md)). |
