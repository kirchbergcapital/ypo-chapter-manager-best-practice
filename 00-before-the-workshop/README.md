# Before the workshop

Purpose: about 30 minutes of preparation, a week before the workshop, so that on the day you spend your time building — not installing or logging in. The workshop runs in the desktop app of ChatGPT or Claude, in one folder on your laptop.
Two prerequisites: a **paid AI plan** with training on your data switched off — if you do not have one yet, start there
([../01-foundations/paid-plan-and-training-off.md](../01-foundations/paid-plan-and-training-off.md), about 20 minutes; which plan, and the check before anyone pays: [../01-foundations/business-plan-checklist.md](../01-foundations/business-plan-checklist.md)) — and **chapter accounts in
Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace** for at least you and your Chair (`manager@`, `chair@`); not there yet? Ask your Chair this week
([../05-officer-handover/role-accounts.md](../05-officer-handover/role-accounts.md)).

> Community resource by a YPO member, not an official YPO resource.

## Two steps, about 30 minutes

| Step | Time | File | You end with |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10 min | [laptop-and-account-check.md](laptop-and-account-check.md) | You know which laptop, storage, mail and AI plan you will use — your one wish, and the question you answer every year for the third time |
| 2 | 20 min | [pre-workshop-selftest.md](pre-workshop-selftest.md) | The desktop app is installed; your working folder `CM-Workspace` with the kit in `_kit` sits in your synced drive; the assistant read a file there and saved one itself; your training setting is checked |

Your answers and your self-test result go to the trainer before the day (a short form or a reply to the pre-workshop message).
They shape the first minutes of the workshop; nobody is asked about plan or self-test in the room.

## Bring on the day

- Your laptop with the desktop app and `CM-Workspace` ready (an iPad works only for the browser path) and its charger
- Your phone — useful as a hotspot, for WhatsApp, and to see that chats and drafts work on the go
- The log-in details for your AI tool and your cloud storage — in your password manager, not on paper
- Your one wish: the task you would most like AI to take off your desk
- Your "third time" question: the one you answer every year when a new officer starts — it feeds your first lab

## If something doesn't work

Come anyway. In the workshop you work next to a neighbour, and there is a way through for each case:

| Situation | On the day |
|---|---|
| No paid AI plan yet | A paid plan is the first prerequisite. Set it up before the workshop (about 20 minutes, see [../01-foundations/paid-plan-and-training-off.md](../01-foundations/paid-plan-and-training-off.md); before anyone pays, the plan pick and check in [../01-foundations/business-plan-checklist.md](../01-foundations/business-plan-checklist.md)). If it is not ready on the day, watch with your neighbour and set it up the same evening. Who pays is an ExCo decision — the one-pager is written for it ([../06-chapter-rules/ai-use-one-pager.md](../06-chapter-rules/ai-use-one-pager.md)) |
| No chapter accounts for you and your Chair yet | Build in your own drive on the day and move the folder into `manager@` once it exists. The request for your Chair is ready in [../05-officer-handover/role-accounts.md](../05-officer-handover/role-accounts.md) |
| No laptop | You pair up with a neighbour and set up your own at home with the kit |
| Your company or chapter IT blocks the tool or the desktop app | Use a private laptop for today, or take the browser path (same labs, you save the files yourself); move everything into the chapter account later — the folder structure moves with it |
| Only an iPad or a Chromebook | The browser path with a neighbour; the desktop app comes on a laptop later |
| The self-test failed | Note where it stopped; we fix it in the first 20 minutes |
