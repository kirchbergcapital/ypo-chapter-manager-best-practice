# 08 · Prompts

Ready-to-use prompts for everyday Chapter Manager work. The other folders hold your files; these prompts put them to work.

## What this is

A small library for the work that comes back every week and every event: following up, onboarding, answering rule questions,
checking the budget, filing mail and WhatsApp, running an event from brief to debrief, keeping the game plan alive, keeping the chapter's memory current, and seeing where your own hours go.
Every prompt says what it reads, which file it produces, and the one check you do yourself.

## Why it matters

The same prompt, used every time, gives you comparable results — and a result that lands in a file stays with the chapter.
That is how a few minutes per task add up to a knowledge base a stand-in or the next officer can continue from.

## What's inside

| File | What it gives you |
|---|---|
| [prompt-library.md](prompt-library.md) | 40 prompts in nine groups: A Events · B Members · C Rules and meetings · D Finance · E Communication · F Memory and handover · G Event lifecycle · H Game plan · I Time by area — and how to share a prompt of your own |

Start with three: **11 Email handoff**, **8 Post-event debrief** and **13 Checkpoint**. Add the others when the need comes up.

## How to use it with your AI

1. **First session:** *"Read 08-prompts/prompt-library.md. Which three prompts would save me the most time this month, given 00-handover/handover.md?"*
2. **Save what works:** copy your favourite prompts, with your own placeholders filled in, into your chapter folder's `08-prompts/` — your assistant prefers them.
3. **Improve them:** when a prompt needed a correction twice, change the saved version, and add a log line saying why.
4. **Share them:** a prompt that works for you may work for other chapters — see "Share a prompt" at the end of [prompt-library.md](prompt-library.md).

## Time needed

| Task | Time |
|---|---|
| Email handoff (11) | 2–5 minutes per mail |

| Goal status (19) and quarterly report (20) | Part of a 30-minute quarterly slot |
| Hours line round-up (24) | 2 minutes at the end of a day |

Numbers stay stable from version to version; new prompts get the next number, so some groups are not in sequence.
Replace every `{{PLACEHOLDER}}`, make sure the files named under "Reads" are in your project or folder, paste the prompt, then run "Your check".

## A · Events

### 1 · Event follow-up
**Purpose:** after an event, thank people and close the open loops in one sitting. **When:** within two days after the event.
**Reads:** `03-events/{{EVENT_FOLDER}}/` (`run-of-show.md`, `rsvps.md`) · `chapter-facts.md`.
```text
Read 03-events/{{EVENT_FOLDER}}/ and 02-chapter-knowledge/chapter-facts.md.
1) Draft a short thank-you to the attendees (one group mail I send myself) and a separate one to the speaker and the host.
2) List open loops: invoices not yet received, promised follow-ups, materials to share. Each with owner (role) and due date.
3) One log line for 00-handover/log.md.
Facts only from the files. Anything missing: [verify]. Drafts only.
```
**Result file:** drafts in `06-communication/drafts/YYYY-MM-DD_event-follow-up_{{EVENT}}.md` · open loops in `03-events/{{EVENT_FOLDER}}/follow-up.md`.
**Your check:** the speaker's name and title match the event folder, not the email signature.

### 6 · Speaker research
**Purpose:** a one-page view of a speaker candidate before the Learning Officer decides. **When:** while planning the learning calendar.
**Reads:** `02-chapter-knowledge/speakers.md` (past speakers and ratings) · the candidate's public website.
```text
Speaker candidate: {{NAME}}, topic {{TOPIC}}.
1) From 02-chapter-knowledge/speakers.md: have we had this speaker or this topic before? Ratings and year.
2) From public sources: background, recent talks, languages — each line with its link.
3) Fees and availability: only if a source states them, marked [verify]. Never estimate.
4) Three questions the Learning Officer could ask in a first call.
One page. Say what you could not check.
```
**Result file:** `03-events/{{EVENT_FOLDER}}/YYYY-MM-DD_speaker-research_{{SPEAKER}}.md`.
**Your check:** open two of the links yourself. A fee without a source stays `[verify]`.

### 7 · Sponsor renewal drafts
**Purpose:** renewal mails that remember what each sponsor got last year. **When:** two to three months before the sponsor year ends.
**Reads:** `05-finance/` sponsor agreements · `03-events/` folders where sponsors appeared · `chapter-facts.md`.
```text
For each sponsor agreement in 05-finance/ that ends before {{DATE}}:
1) What the agreement covered, and which events they actually appeared at (from 03-events/). Source per line.
2) A short, warm renewal draft to the sponsor contact (role from the agreement), with one concrete thank-you.
3) A table: sponsor · ends · value · renewal draft ready · open question for the Chair.
Amounts only from the agreement. Drafts only.
```
**Result file:** `06-communication/drafts/YYYY-MM-DD_sponsor-renewal_{{SPONSOR}}.md` · table in `05-finance/YYYY-MM-DD_sponsor-renewals.md`.
**Your check:** every amount matches the signed agreement; the Chair agrees before anything goes out.

### 8 · Post-event debrief
**Purpose:** the next organiser starts from what you learned, not from zero. **When:** the week after each event, 30 minutes.
**Reads:** your notes or voice memo transcript · `03-events/{{EVENT_FOLDER}}/` · `02-chapter-knowledge/learnings-{{ROLE}}.md`.
```text
Here are my notes from {{EVENT}}. Read 03-events/{{EVENT_FOLDER}}/ and 02-chapter-knowledge/learnings-{{ROLE}}.md.
1) Write a debrief: what worked · what did not · what we change next time · numbers (attendance, cost per head — only from the files).
2) Append three to five learnings to learnings-{{ROLE}}.md: dated, one line each, with the event as source. Do not change older lines.
3) If the event playbook in 02-chapter-knowledge should change, show me the change first.
4) One log line for 00-handover/log.md.
```
**Result file:** `03-events/{{EVENT_FOLDER}}/debrief.md` · new lines at the bottom of `02-chapter-knowledge/learnings-{{ROLE}}.md`.
**Your check:** the learnings are things you would tell the next organiser. Older lines unchanged.

## B · Members

### 5 · New member onboarding
**Purpose:** a warm, complete welcome in ten minutes. **When:** the day a new member is confirmed.
**Reads:** `04-members-and-prospects/` onboarding steps · `chapter-facts.md` · the welcome template in `06-communication/templates/` ([`../03-communication/message-templates.md`](../03-communication/message-templates.md), template 4).
```text
New member: {{FIRST_NAME}} {{LAST_NAME}}, joined {{DATE}}.
1) Fill the welcome template from chapter-facts.md: next event, Forum Officer role, my channel.
2) Mentor: suggest a mentor only from notes the chapter allows in 04-members-and-prospects/. If there are none, write "mentor: Chair to decide".
3) A checklist from our onboarding steps with owner (role) and due date.
4) One log line. Draft only — I send.
```
**Result file:** `06-communication/drafts/YYYY-MM-DD_welcome_new-member.md` · checklist in `04-members-and-prospects/onboarding/YYYY-MM-DD_{{INITIALS}}.md`.
**Your check:** name spelling from the confirmation, not from the email address. No private details beyond what the role needs.

### 31 · Membership pipeline step
**Purpose:** the stage of a prospect or a membership move, and a follow-up draft. **When:** a step changes, or you plan follow-ups.
**Reads:** `04-members-and-prospects/pipeline.md` · `chapter-facts.md` · onboarding steps in that folder, if you keep them · the note you paste.
```text
Update 04-members-and-prospects/pipeline.md from the note I paste. If the file is missing, show me the empty table and the path.
One row per person - add a new row for a new prospect: initials, type (new, transfer, secondary, gold, leaving, or [verify]),
stage (first contact, invited, applied, approved, onboarding, done, or [verify]), next step, due, owner (role), source.
No home address, no private phone, no health or family details. Do not copy the member directory. Link to YPO Connect.
If that step is already on the row, say so.
If a screen asks for these fields, take them from platform-fields.md (prompt 30). Never invent a value.
Draft the next follow-up, one person per draft. I send it.
Anything the source does not state: [verify]. Change the file only after I say yes.
If the note contains Forum content, stop.
---
{{PASTE_NOTE}}
```
**Result file:** `04-members-and-prospects/pipeline.md` · draft in `06-communication/drafts/YYYY-MM-DD_pipeline-follow-up_{{initials}}.md` (initials in lower case)
**Your check:** the stage matches your note; private details are absent; you send the draft.

### 34 · Renewal follow-up list
**Purpose:** one renewal list from a fresh export, kind reminder drafts for the right people, and a status for the ExCo. **When:** renewal opens, before each reminder round, and before the ExCo meeting. Page: [`../11-chapter-moments/renewals-and-dues.md`](../11-chapter-moments/renewals-and-dues.md).
**Reads:** the renewal export or report saved unchanged in `07-reporting/`, with its date · the finance officer's list for chapter dues, if any · `04-members-and-prospects/` (including `pipeline.md`) · `chapter-facts.md` · the renewal dates in `01-role-and-calendar/`.
```text
Reply in the chat first. Create or change a file only after I say yes.
Read the renewal export {{FILE_IN_07_REPORTING}} (as of {{EXPORT_DATE}}), chapter-facts.md, pipeline.md and the renewal dates in 01-role-and-calendar/.
1) Propose a table for 04-members-and-prospects/YYYY-MM-DD_renewals.md: initials · status exactly as the export states it, as of {{EXPORT_DATE}} ·
   called by (role, from my list: {{MEMBERS_OFFICERS_WILL_CALL}}) · next step · due. A status the export does not show: UNCHECKED.
   This table is my working file. Do not copy contact details or the member directory.
2) Reminder drafts, one person per draft, naming no other member - only for members whose status in this export
   shows the renewal is still open. No draft for anyone renewed, UNCHECKED, called by an officer,
   on a pause list ({{PAUSED_INITIALS}}) or in a transition in pipeline.md.
   Friendly; the deadline and where to renew quoted from the official notice; a fact the notice does not give: [verify].
3) A short ExCo status: renewed · open · being called · UNCHECKED, with the export and its date next to the numbers.
No payment details, no reasons for not renewing. Draft only - I send, one person per message.
```
**Result file:** `04-members-and-prospects/YYYY-MM-DD_renewals.md` · drafts in `06-communication/drafts/`.
**Your check:** every status matches an export from this reminder round; members an officer calls and paused members get no draft.

### 35 · Member transition step
**Purpose:** a member moving to YPO Gold, to another chapter or out of YPO gets a proper thank-you and the next steps, and the chapter's lists follow on the right day. **When:** you hear of the move, and when each step is done. Page: [`../11-chapter-moments/moving-to-gold.md`](../11-chapter-moments/moving-to-gold.md).
**Reads:** `04-members-and-prospects/pipeline.md` · `chapter-facts.md` · the official steps you were given, saved with their date · the chapter lists you name (groups, mail lists, invitations).
```text
Reply in the chat first. Create or change a file only after I say yes.
A member transition: {{INITIALS}}, type {{gold / transfer / leaving / [verify]}}. Official steps: {{FILE_OR_LINK_WITH_DATE}}.
1) Propose the row for 04-members-and-prospects/pipeline.md: type, stage (announced, steps under way, complete, or [verify]),
   next step, due, owner (role), source. If the file is missing, show me the empty table and the path.
2) A thank-you draft for the Chair or Membership Officer to sign, and a short "what happens next" note:
   the official link and its date, and quotes from it - no paraphrase. A step the source does not state: [verify with Regional Manager].
3) If chapter-facts.md shows a role for this member, or an event they host, propose one handover-checklist line (part A) for the vacancy.
   The role stays confirmed in chapter-facts.md until the move is complete; then propose "vacant [verify]" until the Chair confirms a successor.
4) Who else to tell: Forum Officer (logistics only), finance officer (open chapter dues), whoever keeps spouse and partner lists.
5) The chapter lists to update - groups, mail lists, invitations - each marked "pending" until three things are true:
   the move is confirmed complete, the member knows, and the change date is agreed.
No reason for the move, no age, health or family details. Draft only - I send.
```
**Result file:** `04-members-and-prospects/pipeline.md` · drafts in `06-communication/drafts/`.
**Your check:** the official steps carry a date and a source; no list and no role changes before the move is complete.

## C · Rules and meetings

### 2 · Bylaws quick answer
**Purpose:** answer "how do we do X?" from the chapter's own documents, with the place it says so. **When:** any rule question from an officer or a member.
**Reads:** bylaws, operating guidelines and `FAQ.md` in `02-chapter-knowledge/`.
```text
Question: {{QUESTION}}
Answer only from the bylaws, guidelines and FAQ.md in 02-chapter-knowledge/.
Give: the short answer · the quote · document name, section and page.
If the documents do not answer it, say so and write [verify with Regional Manager]. Do not answer from general knowledge.
If the answer is new, propose one line for FAQ.md.
```
**Result file:** answer in the chat or as a draft · new line in `02-chapter-knowledge/FAQ.md` once you confirm it.
**Your check:** open the quoted section yourself before you pass the answer on.

### 3 · Meeting minutes → action items
**Purpose:** from an ExCo meeting to a clear list of who does what by when. **When:** the day of the meeting.
**Reads:** your notes or the transcript (recorded only if everyone agreed) · `chapter-facts.md`.
```text
Here are my notes from the ExCo meeting on {{DATE}}.
1) Action items as a table: Who (role) | What | Deadline. Only items someone agreed to; unclear owner = "owner open".
2) Decisions: only items put to the ExCo and agreed. Everything else is "proposed" or "open".
3) A short minutes draft for the Chair to approve.
4) One line per decision for 00-handover/decisions.md; one log line.
Leave out personal stories and anything confidential.
```
**Result file:** `07-reporting/YYYY-MM-DD_exco-minutes.md` · new lines in `00-handover/decisions.md`.
**Your check:** every "decided" line was really put to everyone. If unsure, it is "proposed".

### 28 · Procedure change summary
**Purpose:** a new procedure, best practice or training turned into one short note: what changes for you, by when, and which of your files to update — so your knowledge base stays the one place you look things up. **When:** whenever new guidance, a changed process or training material arrives. **Worth it when** the material is long, changes several steps, or touches your playbooks and deadlines. A short update you can read in a minute needs no prompt.
**Reads:** the new material (mail, PDF, slides, a recording's transcript) · `02-chapter-knowledge/` · `01-role-and-calendar/`.
```text
Here is new or changed guidance: {{PASTE_OR_ATTACH}} (from: {{ROLE — e.g. regional office, global YPO}}, dated {{DATE}}).
Read 02-chapter-knowledge/ and 01-role-and-calendar/ first.
1) What changes, in at most five lines: what I do differently, from when, and what stays as it is. Quote the sentence
   each line rests on. Anything the material leaves open: [verify with Regional Manager].
2) Which of my files this touches - guidelines, playbooks, FAQ.md, the deadlines in 01-role-and-calendar - with the exact
   change for each, shown as before → after. Change nothing yet. If no playbook exists yet for this process, offer to start one in 02-chapter-knowledge/.
3) What is new compared with what I already do, and what only repeats it - so I can see what really needs my time.
4) Any deadline or step that collides with this chapter year's events or the game plan: name it.
   For a new deadline or a training I must attend, offer one row for 01-role-and-calendar/incoming-work.md (prompt 29).
After my yes: if you can write files in my folder, make the changes and add one line to 00-handover/log.md with the
source and the date; otherwise give me each changed file in full to download, and the log line to paste.
Draft only for anything that would go to officers or members.
```
**Result file:** the note in the chat · updated files in `02-chapter-knowledge/` and `01-role-and-calendar/` after your yes (at Level 1 as downloads you save) · one line in `00-handover/log.md`.
**Your check:** every "changes" line has its quoted sentence; nothing in your files changed before your yes.

### 36 · Nominations timeline
**Purpose:** the nominating process and the election run on time and by your bylaws, with every message drafted and a clean record. **When:** when your bylaws say the process opens, or when your Chair asks for the timeline. Page: [`../11-chapter-moments/nominations-and-elections.md`](../11-chapter-moments/nominations-and-elections.md).
**Reads:** the bylaws in `02-chapter-knowledge/` · `chapter-facts.md` · `01-role-and-calendar/` (commitments calendar) · any notice from the region.
```text
Reply in the chat first. Create or change a file only after I say yes.
Read the bylaws in 02-chapter-knowledge/, chapter-facts.md and 01-role-and-calendar/.
1) Quote, word for word, the bylaws sentences on nominations and elections with their section numbers - only text you can see in the file.
   For each of these, quote it or write "not in the bylaws - [verify with Regional Manager]": who leads, who takes part,
   who may nominate, who may vote, which majority, when the term starts.
2) A timeline table: step (call for nominations, deadline, slate, vote, announcement, start of term) · date · owner (role) · source · status.
   A date only where a quoted sentence or a notice gives it, or gives a rule and I give the anchor date ({{ANCHOR_DATE}}); otherwise [verify].
3) Drafts for the call, the reminder, the voting notice and the announcement - each marked "to be approved by {{COMMITTEE_OR_CHAIR}}".
4) For chapter-facts.md: incoming officers stay provisional until the result is confirmed - then confirmed with date and source.
Never suggest, rank or comment on candidates. No individual ballots, raw ballot results or vote counts:
use only the election outcome the committee confirmed, with its date and source. Draft only - I send after approval.
```
**Result file:** `01-role-and-calendar/YYYY-MM-DD_nominations-timeline.md` · drafts in `06-communication/drafts/`.
**Your check:** every date traces to a quoted sentence or a notice; no name is confirmed before the result is.

### 38 · Name change inventory
**Purpose:** after a decided merger or name change, every place that carries the old name is on one plan with an owner and a date. **When:** once the decision is announced, then weekly until done. Page: [`../11-chapter-moments/name-change.md`](../11-chapter-moments/name-change.md).
**Reads:** the chapter folder · `chapter-facts.md` · `02-chapter-knowledge/platform-fields.md` · the decision and the official name, as you paste them.
```text
Reply in the chat first. Create or change a file only after I say yes.
Decision: {{OLD_NAME}} becomes {{NEW_NAME}} ({{MERGER_OR_RENAME}}), source: {{DECISION_SOURCE}}.
1) Search the chapter folder for the old name and its short forms ({{SHORT_FORMS}}). List each file path and field - do not copy text
   from logs, complaints or member files. Include templates, signatures, chapter-facts.md, platform-fields.md, the event template, the AI-use one-pager.
2) Add the places a folder cannot show, as rows for me to confirm: mail addresses and role accounts, the domain, WhatsApp group names,
   Canva, letterheads, social media, the instructions field of my AI tool, the bank account name (finance officer),
   and - if the chapter is a legal entity - register, tax office, contracts, insurance.
3) A change plan: place · old · new · owner (role) · date · status. Mark a row "YPO" only if the decision or the Regional Manager says so.
4) A short announcement draft for the Chair (both Chairs in a merger) and notes for speakers, venues and sponsors with open events.
In a merger: never mix the two chapters' facts silently - where they differ, write both and [verify].
Before the first change, propose a dated copy of the folder as it is for 09-archive/. Never change the old name in logs or in 09-archive/.
Draft only - I send after approval.
```
**Result file:** `01-role-and-calendar/YYYY-MM-DD_name-change-plan.md` · drafts in `06-communication/drafts/`.
**Your check:** every row has an owner and a date; the inventory names places, it does not copy text.

## D · Finance

### 4 · Budget check by category
**Purpose:** see early where the year's budget runs tight. **When:** monthly, and before every ExCo.
**Reads:** `05-finance/{{BUDGET_FILE}}` · `05-finance/receipts.csv`.
```text
Read 05-finance/{{BUDGET_FILE}} and 05-finance/receipts.csv.
1) A table per budget category: budget · spent · committed · left · % used.
2) Flag every category above 90 %.
3) List receipts without a category and amounts that do not match an invoice.
4) End with a count: how many rows you read, how many you could not match.
Numbers only from the files. No forecast unless I ask.
```
**Result file:** `07-reporting/YYYY-MM-DD_budget-check.md`.
**Your check:** compare two categories with the budget file yourself before the table goes to the ExCo.

### 10 · Receipt photo → CSV row
**Purpose:** receipts filed in seconds, not on a Sunday evening. **When:** the day you pay something.
**Reads:** the photo of the receipt · `05-finance/receipts.csv` · budget categories.
```text
Here is a photo of a receipt.
1) One CSV row for 05-finance/receipts.csv: date, vendor, amount, currency, VAT if shown, budget category, event, paid by, file name of the photo.
2) If a field is unreadable, write [verify] — do not guess amounts.
3) If this pushes a category above 90 %, say so.
```
**Result file:** a new row at the bottom of `05-finance/receipts.csv` · photo in `05-finance/` as `YYYY-MM-DD_receipt_{{VENDOR}}.jpg`.
**Your check:** you compare the amount with the photo. Always — the assistant reads, you confirm.

## E · Communication

### 11 · Email handoff
**Purpose:** mail in → filed → logged → drafted. **When:** two to three times a week. Full guide: [`../03-communication/email-handoff.md`](../03-communication/email-handoff.md).
**Reads:** the mail (pasted, or via the mail connector) · `chapter-facts.md` · the bylaws section if the mail asks about a rule. The same prompt as Lab B in [`../02-your-assistant/lab-build-your-assistant.md`](../02-your-assistant/lab-build-your-assistant.md); no rule involved → write `no rules needed` under the second `---`.
```text
New email below, followed by our rules. 1) Say which folder it belongs in and give a file name with its extension (YYYY-MM-DD_sender-role_topic.md).
2) Give me one log line for 00-handover/log.md.
3) Draft a reply in my voice; answer only from the rules below and cite the section; mark every fact without a source as [verify]. I send it myself.
---
{{PASTE_EMAIL}}
---
```

# Complaints and disagreements

Purpose: when a member complains — about an event, a cost, a decision, a person — or two members disagree, the member hears back quickly, the right person takes it over, and the chapter keeps a short, fair record.

## Who does what

| Yours | The Chair's (or the officer your bylaws name) | Not for the chapter folder |
|---|---|---|
| A short, friendly acknowledgement the same or the next working day | Deciding what happens, talking to the people involved, any answer on the substance | Opinions about people, guesses about motives, anything said in confidence |
| A facts note for the Chair: what, when, which event or decision, what was asked | Bringing it to the ExCo, if needed; deciding where the facts note is kept | Forum content — it stays in Forum (house rule 17) |
| Filing the mail, one log line, a reminder until it is closed | Telling the member the outcome, or giving you the words to send | Screenshots of private chats |

**Where it goes:**
- About an event, a cost or a decision → your Chair.
- About the Chair → the officer your bylaws name for this, or ask your Regional Manager who receives it.
- About conduct, harassment or safety → your Chair and your Regional Manager the same day; YPO has its own process for this `[verify with Regional Manager]`.
- About a Forum matter → your Forum Officer; nothing goes into the assistant or the chapter folder.
- About you → hand it to your Chair unchanged and keep your own note outside the chapter folder.

## Steps

1. **Acknowledge with your own timing.** "Thank you for telling us. I will pass this to the right person and let you know by {{A_DATE_YOU_CAN_KEEP}} who is taking it up." No view on the substance, no date or outcome in the Chair's name.
2. **Write the facts note.** One page, roles and initials instead of stories: what happened, when, which event or decision, what the member asks for, which files matter. Mark anything you have not seen yourself as *reported*.
3. **Hand it over.** Send the note to your Chair through your one channel ([`../03-communication/one-channel.md`](../03-communication/one-channel.md)) and agree who replies and by when.
4. **Tell the member who has it.** A second short message with the role and the date the Chair agreed — in the Chair's words, once the Chair has given them.
5. **Keep the thread.** One log line when it arrives, one when it is handed over, one when it is closed. A decision goes into `decisions.md` with the role that decided. The facts note is not filed in the shared chapter folder: it goes to the Chair, and the Chair decides where it is kept.
6. **Learn once.** If the complaint points at a process — late invitations, unclear prices — add a line to the learnings file of that event or process, without the person.

## Good to know

Paste the member's message into your assistant, or — if it holds Forum content or private details — write a short note of your own instead: initials or role, date, event or decision, what is asked. The facts note is for you and the Chair; it stays with you two — outside the shared chapter folder — until the Chair decides who is told what.

## With your assistant

Prompt **32 Complaint first response**: the acknowledgement draft, the facts note and the log line — you check and send.

## Your check

The acknowledgement promises only your own next step and your own date. The facts note is fair if either side read it.

# Moving to Gold — and other member transitions

Purpose: when a member moves on — to YPO Gold, to another chapter, or out of YPO — the chapter says thank you properly, the member knows the next step, and the chapter's lists follow on the right day.

> Who can move to Gold or transfer, when and how: YPO's rules — your Regional Manager and the official resources have the current ones.

## Who does what

| Yours | The Chair's or Membership Officer's (or the officer your bylaws name for membership) | YPO's |
|---|---|---|
| A row in `pipeline.md` for the move: type, stage, next step, owner | The personal conversation and the thank-you on behalf of the chapter | The formal steps of the transition |
| A thank-you draft and a short "what happens next" note: the official link, its date, and quotes — no paraphrase | Deciding how the chapter marks it — a word at an event, a small gift, nothing | The records in YPO's systems, as your Regional Manager describes |
| Updating the chapter's own lists on the agreed day: groups, mail lists, invitations | Asking a successor for any role the member held | |

**Also tell:** the Forum Officer (a seat or headcount changes — logistics only; any Forum role is the Forum Officer's to fill) · the finance officer (open chapter dues) · whoever looks after spouse and partner lists — ask before anyone is removed.

## Steps

1. **Start a row.** In `04-members-and-prospects/pipeline.md`: initials, type (`gold`, `transfer`, `leaving`, or `[verify]`), stage, next step, due, owner (role), source.
2. **Get the official steps.** From your Regional Manager or the official resources — saved as a link or a file with its date.
3. **Thank, then inform.** The Chair or Membership Officer thanks the member personally. You draft the written thank-you and the "what happens next" note.
4. **Plan any handover.** If the member holds a role or was due to host an event, put the vacancy on the handover checklist ([`../05-officer-handover/handover-checklist.md`](../05-officer-handover/handover-checklist.md), part A) as its own line. In `chapter-facts.md` the member stays `confirmed` in the role until the move is complete; then the role is `vacant [verify]` until the Chair confirms a successor.
5. **Tidy the lists, on the agreed day.** Chapter groups, mail lists, invitations — only when the move is complete and the member knows. If YPO's access for the member ends earlier, tell your Chair. The receiving chapter may welcome a warm introduction: draft it if your Chair asks.

## Good to know

Why a member moves on is their story. Age, health and family reasons stay out of the assistant and the folder — the row says the type and the stage.

## With your assistant

Prompt **35 Member transition step**: the proposed pipeline row, the thank-you and next-step drafts and the lists to update — you check and send.

## Your check

The official steps carry a date and a source. Nobody leaves a group, and no role changes, before the move is complete.

# Merger or name change

Purpose: when the chapter merges with another chapter or takes a new name, everything that carries the old name is found, changed on a plan, and nothing important is lost — the mailboxes, the files, the groups, the history.

> How a merger or a rename is decided and approved: your bylaws and YPO — your Regional Manager has the process. This page starts once the decision is announced.

## Who does what

| Yours | The Chair's (both Chairs, in a merger), after the approval your bylaws require | YPO's |
|---|---|---|
| The inventory: every place the old name lives, with an owner and a date | Agreeing the plan and the announcement | The formal steps and the official name |
| The change plan and its status | Decisions where two chapters do things differently | Changes in YPO's own systems |
| Drafts for members, speakers, venues and sponsors | Approving every outward text | |
| Keeping the history readable: dated archives of the old folders | | |

**In a merger** the other chapter often has its own Chapter Manager: they are your counterpart for the inventory. Your own role in the new chapter is a conversation between you and your Chair — not part of this plan.

## Steps

1. **Inventory first.** Your assistant lists every file and field in the chapter folder that carries the old name — templates, signatures, `chapter-facts.md`, `platform-fields.md`, the event template, the AI-use one-pager. You add what the folder cannot see: mail addresses and role accounts, the domain, WhatsApp group names, Canva, letterheads, social media, the instructions field of your AI tool, and — with the finance officer — the bank account name.
2. **Plan with owners and dates.** One row per place: old · new · owner (role) · date · status. A row is marked `YPO` only where the decision or your Regional Manager says YPO changes it — you leave those alone.
3. **If the chapter is a legal entity** (for example a registered association), the name also sits in the register, with the tax office, the bank, contracts and insurance: one row each, owner the finance officer or the chapter's legal adviser.
4. **Keep the history.** Before the first change, a dated snapshot copy of the folder exactly as it is goes into `09-archive/` (in a merger: one per chapter). The working folder itself stays where it is, so shared links and permissions keep working — this snapshot is the one place the kit copies instead of moving. Then you change the working folder. Logs and archived records keep the old name — they are history. In a merger, the new `chapter-facts.md` is built only from confirmed facts; where the two chapters differ, write both and mark `[verify]` until the Chairs decide.
5. **Tell people once.** One approved announcement to members, then short notes to speakers, venues and sponsors with open events. Old addresses forward for an agreed time.
6. **Close it.** A line in `decisions.md` with the decision and its source, a line in `log.md` when the last row is done, and a check one month later: does anything still show the old name?

## Good to know

The inventory names the place and the field — it does not copy text out of logs, complaints or member files. What led to the decision stays with the Chairs.

## With your assistant

Prompt **38 Name change inventory**: the inventory, the change plan and the announcement drafts — you check, the Chairs approve, you send.

## Your check

Every row has an owner and a date. Nothing in `09-archive/` was changed after it was archived.

# Newsletter and year review

Purpose: a member newsletter or the review of the chapter year comes together in an hour instead of a weekend — written from the chapter's own files, approved by the Chair, and true in every number.

## Who does what

| Yours | The Chair's and officers' | Not for the newsletter |
|---|---|---|
| Collecting from the files you name: past events, upcoming dates, new members as announced, goals, thank-yous | The Chair's own words — a short note at the top | Anything a member has not agreed to see in print |
| A first draft, in the chapter's voice and length | Approving the whole text | Forum content, private news, numbers without a source |
| Photos only from events whose photo rules allow it | Choosing who gets a special thank-you | Member data copied from YPO Connect |

## A newsletter (monthly or quarterly)

1. **Name the period and the files.** For example: events since {{DATE}}, the event folders of the next three months, the welcomes already sent, the dates members need from `incoming-work.md`.
2. **Draft short.** Three to five items: what happened (one line each, a photo if allowed), what is next (date, place, link), one thank-you, one ask. A space for the Chair's note.
3. **Approve, then send.** The Chair approves; you send it from the chapter's mail tool — and a short post pointing to it, if that is your custom.

## The year review

1. **Collect the year.** Events with attendance from the check-in list or the debrief — if you only have RSVPs, it says "registered". Game plan goals as written in `game-plan.md`, with status and evidence (prompt 19). Officers and thank-yous from `chapter-facts.md`.
2. **Draft in three parts.** What we did · what we learned · what comes next. Every number names its file.
3. **Two versions.** The member version is warm and short. The internal version for the next ExCo also keeps the honest learnings — it goes into `07-reporting/` and the handover. Learnings never go into the member version unchanged.

For a design: the text goes into Canva ([`../07-connectors-and-browser/canva.md`](../07-connectors-and-browser/canva.md)).

## Good to know

Private news appears only if the member shared it for the newsletter. A number without a source becomes `[verify]`, not a guess.

## With your assistant

Prompt **37 Newsletter or year review draft** — you check, the Chair approves, you send.

## Your check

Every number names its file. Every person named has agreed or was already announced. "Registered" and "attended" are not mixed up.

# Nominations and elections

Purpose: the nominating process and the election of the next officers run on time, by the chapter's own rules, with a clean record — so the incoming officers are ready when their term starts.

> The process comes from your bylaws and from YPO's requirements: who nominates, who may vote, which majority, which deadlines, when the term starts. Read both; where they differ or are unclear, `[verify with Regional Manager]`. The body your bylaws name — often a Nominating Committee — leads.

## Who does what

| Yours | The Nominating Committee's or Chair's (as your bylaws name them) | Not yours |
|---|---|---|
| The timeline, built from the bylaws and the official notices | Asking candidates, building the slate, every conversation about people | Suggesting, ranking or commenting on candidates |
| Drafts: the call, the reminder, the voting notice, the announcement | Approving every text before it goes out | Counting a vote alone, or deciding a tie |
| The record: who was nominated (as the committee confirms it), the result, the date, the source | Confirming the result | Vote counts per person, unless the bylaws make them public |
| Starting the handover for every role that changes | | |

Ask once, early, who in your bylaws takes part — for example the Chair-Elect or the outgoing Chair — and who may nominate and vote. Not in the bylaws: `[verify with Regional Manager]`.

## Steps

1. **Find the rules.** Your assistant quotes the bylaws sections on nominations and elections — only text it can see in the file, with the section number (prompt 2). Missing: it says so.
2. **Build the timeline when the process opens.** Each step with a date and an owner (role). A date goes in only where the bylaws or a notice give it, or give a rule plus a date you supply; otherwise `[verify]`. YPO's own deadline for naming incoming officers belongs in your commitments calendar ([`../05-officer-handover/commitments-calendar.md`](../05-officer-handover/commitments-calendar.md)) `[verify with Regional Manager]`.
3. **Draft the messages.** Call for nominations, reminder, voting notice, announcement — each approved by the committee or the Chair, sent by you. If you set up an online ballot, the raw results go to the committee only — never into the assistant. Your assistant works only with the outcome the committee confirmed.
4. **Provisional until confirmed.** In `chapter-facts.md` the incoming officers stay `provisional` until the result is confirmed; then `confirmed`, with the date and the source (house rule 7).
5. **Record, report, hand over.** One line in `decisions.md` with the result and who confirmed it. Ask your Regional Manager how the new officers get into YPO's records and what YPO needs for their officer training `[verify with Regional Manager]`. Then start the officer briefings (prompt 9) and the handover checklist ([`../05-officer-handover/handover-checklist.md`](../05-officer-handover/handover-checklist.md)).

## Good to know

Discussions about candidates and reasons someone declined stay with the committee. The record says who was elected, when, under which rule.

## With your assistant

Prompt **36 Nominations timeline**: the bylaws quotes, the timeline and the message drafts — you check, the committee approves, you send.

## Your check

Every date traces to a bylaws sentence or a notice you can point at. No name is `confirmed` before the result is.

# Renewals and dues

Purpose: in renewal season, one list shows who has renewed, who has not, and who needs a friendly word — so your officers can act early, and nobody gets the same reminder three times.

> YPO rules for renewals and dues: your Regional Manager and the official resources. Chapter dues, if your chapter has them: your bylaws and the officer they name for finance.

## Who does what

| Yours | The Membership Officer's, finance officer's or Chair's (as your bylaws name them) | YPO's |
|---|---|---|
| The renewal list: status from the source, as of a date, the next step | Personal conversations with members who hesitate | Renewal rules, invoices, deadlines, grace periods, exceptions |
| Reminder drafts in the chapter's voice — one person per draft | Payment questions, chapter dues, any exception at chapter level | What happens after a deadline |
| Dates in the chapter calendar: renewal opens, reminders, deadline | Telling you which members they will call themselves | |

## Steps

1. **Put the dates in.** When renewal opens, the deadline, and your reminder dates go into `01-role-and-calendar/` — from the official notice or your Regional Manager, never from memory.
2. **Build the list from a fresh source.** The status comes from YPO's report or the platform export, saved unchanged in `07-reporting/` with its date. Your list in `04-members-and-prospects/` uses initials, the status as the source states it, *as of* that date, the next step and the owner (role). Chapter dues come from the finance officer's own list.
3. **Agree who speaks to whom.** Your Membership Officer and your Chair pick the members they will call. Reminder drafts go only to the others whose renewal the export shows as still open — never to anyone `UNCHECKED`, on a pause after sad news, or in the middle of a transition (prompt 35).
4. **Refresh, then remind.** Before each reminder round, save a new export: whoever paid yesterday gets no reminder today. First a friendly reminder with the deadline and where to renew; then — only if agreed — a personal note from an officer. One person per message, never a group message.
5. **Report the picture.** A short status for the ExCo: renewed, open, being called, `UNCHECKED` — with the export and its date next to the numbers.

## Good to know

Reasons a member gives for not renewing are personal: they go to the officer in a call, not into the assistant. The renewal list is your working file — it is not sent to members or posted anywhere. No payment or bank details in the folder.

## With your assistant

Prompt **34 Renewal follow-up list**: the list from the saved export, the reminder drafts and the ExCo status — you check and send.

## Your check

Every status matches an export no older than your reminder round. Members an officer calls get no reminder draft.

# Sad news and emergencies

Purpose: when a member, a spouse or partner, or someone close to the chapter dies or is seriously ill, the right people hear it from the right person, in the right order, and nothing automatic reaches the family. When an emergency hits an event, people are safe first and members get one clear, practical message.

These are two different moments with two different paths.

## A · Loss or serious illness

### Who does what

| Yours | The Chair's | Not yours to decide |
|---|---|---|
| Day one: pausing everything automatic that could reach the family or land badly | Whether, when and how the chapter is told; speaking with the family | What the family wants shared — the Chair asks, you follow |
| A draft message for the Chair, in the chapter's voice | Approving and signing the message | Details of illness or circumstances |
| Practical support: a card or flowers if the chapter does that, attendance at a service, a condolence address | Who represents the chapter; telling the Forum Officer | Anything about the person's Forum — the Forum Officer and the Forum handle that themselves |

### Day one

1. **Tell your Chair first.** Do not forward the news to anyone else.
2. **Pause what runs on its own** — until the Chair lifts the pause, not just for a week. Look in every tool, not only the folder:
   - scheduled mails and newsletters in your mail tool,
   - event registrations and reminders in the registration platform — the person's open registrations too,
   - birthday or anniversary automations,
   - scheduled WhatsApp or social posts,
   - open drafts in `06-communication/drafts/` (for example renewal reminders or the next newsletter).
3. **Ask for YPO's mail to pause.** Your Chair or you ask your Regional Manager how to stop YPO's own mail to the member — renewal notices, invitations — and what happens to the record `[verify with Regional Manager]`.
4. **The Forum hears it from its own people.** Your Chair tells the Forum Officer, who reaches the person's Forum. You do not message Forum members.

### The days after

5. **Wait for the family's words.** Your Chair, or whoever is closest, asks what may be shared. Until then nothing goes to members.
6. **Draft for the Chair.** The news exactly in the words the family allowed, one warm sentence, practical details (service, condolence address) only if confirmed. A private home address or phone number you add yourself in the final text, outside the assistant. Signed by the Chair.
7. **Send once.** The approved message goes out as agreed: one mail, and one post in the chapter group only if that is already your custom.
8. **The partner and the family.** If a spouse or partner is also a chapter contact, the Chair decides who writes to them. Partner and family stay on lists and invitations only as the family wishes.
9. **The quiet tasks, later.** A card or flowers if the chapter does that, a note in the chapter calendar so next year's mailings are thoughtful, and the record steps your Regional Manager gave you.

**Serious illness without a death:** a leave of absence or an attendance question goes to the Chair and the Regional Manager. The chapter shares only what the member allows.

## B · Emergency at an event — or a sudden cancellation

1. **Safety first.** At an accident or a medical emergency: emergency services, then the venue's staff. Then your Chair, or the deputy named in `chapter-facts.md`.
2. **Report it.** Your Chair or you tell your Regional Manager the same day; ask what YPO needs to know, for example for insurance `[verify with Regional Manager]`.
3. **One practical message the same day.** A venue cancelled, a speaker stuck, a change of place: what changes, what members do now, who to call. Your Chair approves it if it is more than logistics.
4. **Log it.** One line in the event's folder and in `00-handover/log.md`; learnings after the event.

## What stays out

Cause of death, diagnoses, family circumstances, condolence texts from members. Into the assistant goes only the wording the family allowed and the person's initials; it may look up the name in `chapter-facts.md` only to find drafts and registrations to pause. The name, and any private address, you add in the final text yourself. In the chapter folder: one log line with the date and what the chapter did.

## With your assistant

- Prompt **33 Sad news: pause list and message** — part A on day one, with initials only: the pause list and who tells whom; part B once the Chair has the family's words: the message draft.
- Prompt **39 Event emergency note** — the practical same-day message and the log line.

You check, the Chair approves, you send.

## Your check

Nothing in the draft that the family has not allowed. Every pause stays until the Chair lifts it. One sender, one send.
