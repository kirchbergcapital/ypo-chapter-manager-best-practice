# Do's and don'ts — one page

Purpose: the short list to keep next to your laptop. **As of 23 Nov 2026** — menus move, check once a quarter.
If your chapter has its own AI rules, or YPO sets a line, that applies first.

## 1 · Your account

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Work in a **paid plan**. A business plan in the chapter's name is ideal ([which one](business-plan-checklist.md)) | Put chapter data into a free plan or a private account |
| **Switch training off** before your first real chat, and keep a screenshot. Claude: Settings → Privacy → *Help Improve our AI models* → off. ChatGPT: Settings → Data controls → *Improve the model for everyone* → off ([all tools](paid-plan-and-training-off.md)) | Assume it is off. On personal plans it can be on |
| **Two-step sign-in** (a code on your phone at each login) on the chapter accounts, and on your AI account where it offers it | Share one login. Each person has their own paid login |

## 2 · What goes in

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Chapter work: bylaws, events, budgets, mails, minutes. That is what your assistant is for | **Forum content.** Forum logistics yes (dates, places, headcounts, costs, deadlines). Forum content never |
| Member data your role needs, as YPO's rules allow ([YPO data or chapter data](where-your-data-lives.md#ypo-data-or-chapter-data)) | Passwords, codes, card numbers. They belong in the chapter's password manager |

## 3 · How you work

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| **Drafts only.** Your AI drafts, you read and send ([house rules](../02-your-assistant/template-folder/house-rules.md)) | Let it send mail, WhatsApp messages or posts by itself |
| Check facts against your own files. Ask your AI to mark anything it is unsure of | Publish a statement about a person that nobody has checked |
| Explained something twice? Write it into your house rules, or make it a routine ([routines](../08-prompts/routines.md)) | Explain the same thing again in every new chat |

## 4 · Connectors, plugins, skills — what to add

**Connector** = a link to another service, such as your mailbox. **Plugin** = an add-on package for the Claude desktop app (Cowork). **Skill** = a saved instruction file Claude uses for one kind of task.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| **Good first connectors:** your chapter mailbox (drafts only), calendar, cloud drive, Canva — from your tool's own list ([how](../07-connectors-and-browser/README.md)) | Add a connector by a web address someone sent you. Ask your Chair first |
| **Plugins** from Anthropic's lists in the Claude desktop app (*Customize → Plugins*) | Plugins from other lists. Ask your Chair first |
| **Skills** you wrote yourself, or from Anthropic. Read a skill a colleague shares before you switch it on | Skills downloaded from the internet |
| When a connector asks what it may do, choose **read** or **drafts**. You can add more later | Click "allow all" to make a question go away |
| Anything new goes on the chapter's AI tool list first: one line, OK by the Chair ([the list](../06-chapter-rules/ai-use-one-pager.md)) | Add apps, extensions or automations nobody else knows about |

ChatGPT: the same rules apply to its apps and custom GPTs.

## 5 · Ready for more?

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| **One routine at a time:** first it prepares, then it files, then it does things that are easy to undo. Check what it did once a week | Hand over a whole area at once |
| A scheduled task that works in your folder runs only while your computer is awake and the app is open. For the Monday report, use a computer that stays on | Expect a laptop with a closed lid to do it |
| **Sending always stays with you.** It matters most when your AI reads incoming mail or websites | Let an AI that reads outside mail also send, post or open links by itself |
