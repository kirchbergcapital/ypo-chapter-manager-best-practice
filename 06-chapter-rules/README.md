# 06 · Chapter rules — two short texts for the ExCo to adopt, and a page for your Chair

You work for the chapter — self-employed or employed, never for YPO Global. Either way the chapter decides how it uses AI and how its ExCo works with you.
This folder gives you two ready texts to put in front of your Chair — written so they can be proposed and the
ExCo can adopt them in one meeting — and one page written for the Chair.

| File | What it is | Who decides |
|---|---|---|
| [ai-use-one-pager.md](ai-use-one-pager.md) | **AI use in our chapter** — five rules to adopt now, a separate OK per automation beyond drafting, tools and who pays. One printed page. | Proposed by the Chair and the Chapter Manager, adopted by the ExCo |
| [operating-guardrails.md](operating-guardrails.md) | **How the ExCo and the CM work together** — one channel, lead time, response target, fixed dates, briefing before taking office | Proposed by the Chair, adopted by the ExCo |
| [for-your-chair.md](for-your-chair.md) | **For the Chapter Chair** — one page to hand your Chair: why the CM is the chapter's memory, the five ways of working, the one-pager, thirty minutes at the start of a term | Nothing to adopt — it prepares the conversation |

## How to use them

1. Give your Chair [for-your-chair.md](for-your-chair.md) first — it explains both texts from the Chair's side.
2. **You and your Chair fill the `{{placeholders}}` together**, in a 1:1 before the ExCo meeting — chapter name, date,
   admin role, year, and for the one-pager the tools and who pays. Change what does not fit your chapter — these are
   starting points, not a standard. The Chair puts them on the ExCo agenda and sends them with the invitation.
   Both texts fit on one page each.
3. Once adopted, file them in `02-chapter-knowledge/` with the date of the decision, and add them to every
   new officer's briefing ([../05-officer-handover/officer-briefing-template.md](../05-officer-handover/officer-briefing-template.md)).
4. Review both once a year at the officer handover ([../05-officer-handover/handover-checklist.md](../05-officer-handover/handover-checklist.md), section C).

Both texts are chapter-internal guidelines, not official YPO material. They do not replace YPO's own rules — check with
your Regional Manager.
