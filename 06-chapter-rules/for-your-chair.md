# For the Chapter Chair

Your officers change every year. Your Chapter Manager stays. That is why they are the memory of the chapter.

They work for the chapter — self-employed or as its employee — for the whole ExCo, on their own WhatsApp number. Either way, how they may use AI at work is the chapter's decision; the two texts in this folder make it one. When a board walks in, they are the person who still knows how a decision was made, how an event actually runs, and what is still open. A paid AI plan, pointed at a folder the chapter owns, helps them keep that memory in files the next officer can read — drafts, notes, a handover. The bet: the hours come from searching and answering twice — their log will tell you. The assistant drafts. Your Chapter Manager reads, edits and sends.

## Five ways of working

Agree these for the year. The rules cost no money; they ask for discipline. The text for the ExCo: [operating-guardrails.md](operating-guardrails.md).

1. **One channel.** Requests reach your Chapter Manager through you, or one named person, as a single list in priority order.
2. **Lead time.** Updates and material arrive one week before they are needed.
3. **A response target.** They answer within 24 hours or name a date. The ExCo does the same when they ask for a decision.
4. **Fixed dates.** ExCo dates and deadlines stay put. Event work is budgeted apart from their base agreement.
5. **A briefing before office.** Every new officer sits with your Chapter Manager, and hears the chapter's AI house rules, before they start.

Review the five at the next officer handover.

## The one-pager, with your Chapter Manager

Combine the five with a one-page decision on how the chapter uses AI ([ai-use-one-pager.md](ai-use-one-pager.md)). The page says: Forum logistics yes, Forum content never — a name next to a topic is content (the short form; the page quotes the kit's Forum rule in full); the chapter uses paid AI plans only, with training on its data switched off — a business plan with a data processing agreement where the chapter pays; a human sends every message; chapter work lives in role accounts — at least `manager@` and `chair@` — and in storage the chapter owns; the chapter's own files are what the assistant trusts, and anything unsure is marked for a person to check.

**Who fills it.** Your Chapter Manager drafts it. They know the folder, the addresses and the year. You edit it until it sounds like your chapter, and you bring it in your name.

**When.** Sit together before your first ExCo, or in the first weeks of your term. Then give it one **20-minute ExCo item**: adopt the one-pager and the five ways of working for the year, name who administers the role accounts together with your Chapter Manager, and record the decision in the minutes. If the room wants longer, keep the draft in the chapter folder and bring it to the handover.

## Thirty minutes at the start of your term

Book half an hour with your Chapter Manager in the first weeks. That is the onboarding.

- **Ten minutes** in the chapter folder: where last year's decisions live, and what still sits only in a head or a private mailbox.
- **Ten minutes** on the rhythm: the one list, the one-week lead time, and which dates you will protect.
- **Ten minutes** on the draft: what you will change, and the date of the 20-minute ExCo item.

## What you give them

- **One channel**, so five officers are not five urgent lists.
- **Chapter accounts — required:** Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace with at least `manager@` for your Chapter Manager and `chair@` for you, on the chapter's domain, and cloud storage the chapter owns, with two admins. That is what makes the handover work and lets you read the chapter folder. The address and the folder stay when people change. WhatsApp stays on their own number. What the chapter must keep is written into the folder as a summary, a decision or a file. How to set it up: [role-accounts.md](../05-officer-handover/role-accounts.md).
- **The AI on the same accounts.** Since the two accounts exist anyway, the simplest business plan is Microsoft 365 Business with Copilot or Google Workspace with Gemini on `manager@` and `chair@`. ChatGPT Business or Claude Team can come on top; their two-seat minimum fits your Chapter Manager and you. A business plan in the chapter's name keeps training off by default, comes with a data processing agreement, and lets the chapter add and remove people when roles change. Which plan, and a 10-point check before you buy: [business-plan-checklist.md](../01-foundations/business-plan-checklist.md). Whether your Chapter Manager is self-employed or employed by the chapter, seats paid by the chapter keep chapter work off their personal tools.

## What you need yourself

- **Your own `chair@` account** in the chapter's Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace — not your company or private mail. With it you read the chapter folder, your chapter mail stays with the role, and on handover day your successor gets a password reset instead of a forwarded inbox.
