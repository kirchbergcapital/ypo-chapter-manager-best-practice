# Situations — what is on your desk, and where the kit helps

Purpose: start from the situation you are in, not from the kit's chapters. Find the line that fits, open the file, or give the prompt to your assistant.

> Community resource by a YPO member, not an official YPO resource. Where YPO rules apply, your Regional Manager and the official YPO resources are the reference.

**With your AI:** describe the situation in your own words and add: `Use SITUATIONS.md from the kit to find the right file or prompt.`
Prompt numbers refer to [`08-prompts/prompt-library.md`](08-prompts/prompt-library.md); they stay the same from version to version.
