# Chapter moments

Purpose: the moments that do not come every week, but come every year in some chapter — and then all at once. Each page says what is yours, who decides, what to do first, what stays out of the AI, and which prompt helps.

> Community resource by a YPO member, not an official YPO resource. Where YPO rules apply — dues, membership, Gold, elections, mergers — your Regional Manager and the official YPO resources are the reference, together with your chapter's bylaws; where they differ or are unclear, ask your Regional Manager. The pages never state those rules; they help you with your part.

## The same three lines on every page

1. **You draft, others decide.** The Chair or the officer named in your bylaws decides; you prepare, file and send what they approved. Commitments in anyone's name come from them — house rule 6. Your own messages promise only your own next step.
2. **People first, files second.** In a difficult moment a phone call from the right person comes before any message. Your assistant helps with what comes after: the facts, the dates, the drafts, the log.
3. **Less is more in the AI.** Roles and initials instead of stories; no health, family or private details; Forum content stays out completely (house rule 17).

## How to use it with your AI

Tell your assistant what happened in one or two sentences — for sad news, only initials and the words the family allowed — and add: `Use the page for this moment in 11-chapter-moments/ and the matching prompt.`
The prompts are in [`../08-prompts/prompt-library.md`](../08-prompts/prompt-library.md). Each one answers in the chat first and changes a file only after your yes; every result is a draft, and you send it.

**Also tell — the line people forget on the day:** your Regional Manager (anything YPO needs to know), the finance officer (money), the Forum Officer (Forum logistics only), whoever keeps the spouse and partner lists.
