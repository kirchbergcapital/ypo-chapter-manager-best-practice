# Moving to Gold — and other member transitions

Purpose: when a member moves on — to YPO Gold, to another chapter, or out of YPO — the chapter says thank you properly, the member knows the next step, and the chapter's lists follow on the right day.

> Who can move to Gold or transfer, when and how: YPO's rules — your Regional Manager and the official resources have the current ones.

## Who does what

| Yours | The Chair's or Membership Officer's (or the officer your bylaws name for membership) | YPO's |
|---|---|---|
| A row in `pipeline.md` for the move: type, stage, next step, owner | The personal conversation and the thank-you on behalf of the chapter | The formal steps of the transition |
| A thank-you draft and a short "what happens next" note: the official link, its date, and quotes — no paraphrase | Deciding how the chapter marks it — a word at an event, a small gift, nothing | The records in YPO's systems, as your Regional Manager describes |
| Updating the chapter's own lists on the agreed day: groups, mail lists, invitations | Asking a successor for any role the member held | |

**Also tell:** the Forum Officer (a seat or headcount changes — logistics only; any Forum role is the Forum Officer's to fill) · the finance officer (open chapter dues) · whoever looks after spouse and partner lists — ask before anyone is removed.

## Steps

1. **Start a row.** In `04-members-and-prospects/pipeline.md`: initials, type (`gold`, `transfer`, `leaving`, or `[verify]`), stage, next step, due, owner (role), source.
2. **Get the official steps.** From your Regional Manager or the official resources — saved as a link or a file with its date.
3. **Thank, then inform.** The Chair or Membership Officer thanks the member personally. You draft the written thank-you and the "what happens next" note.
4. **Plan any handover.** If the member holds a role or was due to host an event, put the vacancy on the handover checklist ([`../05-officer-handover/handover-checklist.md`](../05-officer-handover/handover-checklist.md), part A) as its own line. In `chapter-facts.md` the member stays `confirmed` in the role until the move is complete; then the role is `vacant [verify]` until the Chair confirms a successor.
5. **Tidy the lists, on the agreed day.** Chapter groups, mail lists, invitations — only when the move is complete and the member knows. If YPO's access for the member ends earlier, tell your Chair. The receiving chapter may welcome a warm introduction: draft it if your Chair asks.

## Good to know

Why a member moves on is their story. Age, health and family reasons stay out of the assistant and the folder — the row says the type and the stage.

## With your assistant

Prompt **35 Member transition step**: the proposed pipeline row, the thank-you and next-step drafts and the lists to update — you check and send.

## Your check

The official steps carry a date and a source. Nobody leaves a group, and no role changes, before the move is complete.
