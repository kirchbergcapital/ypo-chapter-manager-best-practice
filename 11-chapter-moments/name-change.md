# Merger or name change

Purpose: when the chapter merges with another chapter or takes a new name, everything that carries the old name is found, changed on a plan, and nothing important is lost — the mailboxes, the files, the groups, the history.

> How a merger or a rename is decided and approved: your bylaws and YPO — your Regional Manager has the process. This page starts once the decision is announced.

## Who does what

| Yours | The Chair's (both Chairs, in a merger), after the approval your bylaws require | YPO's |
|---|---|---|
| The inventory: every place the old name lives, with an owner and a date | Agreeing the plan and the announcement | The formal steps and the official name |
| The change plan and its status | Decisions where two chapters do things differently | Changes in YPO's own systems |
| Drafts for members, speakers, venues and sponsors | Approving every outward text | |
| Keeping the history readable: dated archives of the old folders | | |

**In a merger** the other chapter often has its own Chapter Manager: they are your counterpart for the inventory. Your own role in the new chapter is a conversation between you and your Chair — not part of this plan.

## Steps

1. **Inventory first.** Your assistant lists every file and field in the chapter folder that carries the old name — templates, signatures, `chapter-facts.md`, `platform-fields.md`, the event template, the AI-use one-pager. You add what the folder cannot see: mail addresses and role accounts, the domain, WhatsApp group names, Canva, letterheads, social media, the instructions field of your AI tool, and — with the finance officer — the bank account name.
2. **Plan with owners and dates.** One row per place: old · new · owner (role) · date · status. A row is marked `YPO` only where the decision or your Regional Manager says YPO changes it — you leave those alone.
3. **If the chapter is a legal entity** (for example a registered association), the name also sits in the register, with the tax office, the bank, contracts and insurance: one row each, owner the finance officer or the chapter's legal adviser.
4. **Keep the history.** Before the first change, a dated snapshot copy of the folder exactly as it is goes into `09-archive/` (in a merger: one per chapter). The working folder itself stays where it is, so shared links and permissions keep working — this snapshot is the one place the kit copies instead of moving. Then you change the working folder. Logs and archived records keep the old name — they are history. In a merger, the new `chapter-facts.md` is built only from confirmed facts; where the two chapters differ, write both and mark `[verify]` until the Chairs decide.
5. **Tell people once.** One approved announcement to members, then short notes to speakers, venues and sponsors with open events. Old addresses forward for an agreed time.
6. **Close it.** A line in `decisions.md` with the decision and its source, a line in `log.md` when the last row is done, and a check one month later: does anything still show the old name?

## Good to know

The inventory names the place and the field — it does not copy text out of logs, complaints or member files. What led to the decision stays with the Chairs.

## With your assistant

Prompt **38 Name change inventory**: the inventory, the change plan and the announcement drafts — you check, the Chairs approve, you send.

## Your check

Every row has an owner and a date. Nothing in `09-archive/` was changed after it was archived.
