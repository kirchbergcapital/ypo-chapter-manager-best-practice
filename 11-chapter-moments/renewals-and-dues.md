# Renewals and dues

Purpose: in renewal season, one list shows who has renewed, who has not, and who needs a friendly word — so your officers can act early, and nobody gets the same reminder three times.

> YPO rules for renewals and dues: your Regional Manager and the official resources. Chapter dues, if your chapter has them: your bylaws and the officer they name for finance.

## Who does what

| Yours | The Membership Officer's, finance officer's or Chair's (as your bylaws name them) | YPO's |
|---|---|---|
| The renewal list: status from the source, as of a date, the next step | Personal conversations with members who hesitate | Renewal rules, invoices, deadlines, grace periods, exceptions |
| Reminder drafts in the chapter's voice — one person per draft | Payment questions, chapter dues, any exception at chapter level | What happens after a deadline |
| Dates in the chapter calendar: renewal opens, reminders, deadline | Telling you which members they will call themselves | |

## Steps

1. **Put the dates in.** When renewal opens, the deadline, and your reminder dates go into `01-role-and-calendar/` — from the official notice or your Regional Manager, never from memory.
2. **Build the list from a fresh source.** The status comes from YPO's report or the platform export, saved unchanged in `07-reporting/` with its date. Your list in `04-members-and-prospects/` uses initials, the status as the source states it, *as of* that date, the next step and the owner (role). Chapter dues come from the finance officer's own list.
3. **Agree who speaks to whom.** Your Membership Officer and your Chair pick the members they will call. Reminder drafts go only to the others whose renewal the export shows as still open — never to anyone `UNCHECKED`, on a pause after sad news, or in the middle of a transition (prompt 35).
4. **Refresh, then remind.** Before each reminder round, save a new export: whoever paid yesterday gets no reminder today. First a friendly reminder with the deadline and where to renew; then — only if agreed — a personal note from an officer. One person per message, never a group message.
5. **Report the picture.** A short status for the ExCo: renewed, open, being called, `UNCHECKED` — with the export and its date next to the numbers.

## Good to know

Reasons a member gives for not renewing are personal: they go to the officer in a call, not into the assistant. The renewal list is your working file — it is not sent to members or posted anywhere. No payment or bank details in the folder.

## With your assistant

Prompt **34 Renewal follow-up list**: the list from the saved export, the reminder drafts and the ExCo status — you check and send.

## Your check

Every status matches an export no older than your reminder round. Members an officer calls get no reminder draft.
