# Complaints and disagreements

Purpose: when a member complains — about an event, a cost, a decision, a person — or two members disagree, the member hears back quickly, the right person takes it over, and the chapter keeps a short, fair record.

## Who does what

| Yours | The Chair's (or the officer your bylaws name) | Not for the chapter folder |
|---|---|---|
| A short, friendly acknowledgement the same or the next working day | Deciding what happens, talking to the people involved, any answer on the substance | Opinions about people, guesses about motives, anything said in confidence |
| A facts note for the Chair: what, when, which event or decision, what was asked | Bringing it to the ExCo, if needed; deciding where the facts note is kept | Forum content — it stays in Forum (house rule 17) |
| Filing the mail, one log line, a reminder until it is closed | Telling the member the outcome, or giving you the words to send | Screenshots of private chats |

**Where it goes:**
- About an event, a cost or a decision → your Chair.
- About the Chair → the officer your bylaws name for this, or ask your Regional Manager who receives it.
- About conduct, harassment or safety → your Chair and your Regional Manager the same day; YPO has its own process for this `[verify with Regional Manager]`.
- About a Forum matter → your Forum Officer; nothing goes into the assistant or the chapter folder.
- About you → hand it to your Chair unchanged and keep your own note outside the chapter folder.

## Steps

1. **Acknowledge with your own timing.** "Thank you for telling us. I will pass this to the right person and let you know by {{A_DATE_YOU_CAN_KEEP}} who is taking it up." No view on the substance, no date or outcome in the Chair's name.
2. **Write the facts note.** One page, roles and initials instead of stories: what happened, when, which event or decision, what the member asks for, which files matter. Mark anything you have not seen yourself as *reported*.
3. **Hand it over.** Send the note to your Chair through your one channel ([`../03-communication/one-channel.md`](../03-communication/one-channel.md)) and agree who replies and by when.
4. **Tell the member who has it.** A second short message with the role and the date the Chair agreed — in the Chair's words, once the Chair has given them.
5. **Keep the thread.** One log line when it arrives, one when it is handed over, one when it is closed. A decision goes into `decisions.md` with the role that decided. The facts note is not filed in the shared chapter folder: it goes to the Chair, and the Chair decides where it is kept.
6. **Learn once.** If the complaint points at a process — late invitations, unclear prices — add a line to the learnings file of that event or process, without the person.

## Good to know

Paste the member's message into your assistant, or — if it holds Forum content or private details — write a short note of your own instead: initials or role, date, event or decision, what is asked. The facts note is for you and the Chair; it stays with you two — outside the shared chapter folder — until the Chair decides who is told what.

## With your assistant

Prompt **32 Complaint first response**: the acknowledgement draft, the facts note and the log line — you check and send.

## Your check

The acknowledgement promises only your own next step and your own date. The facts note is fair if either side read it.
