# Nominations and elections

Purpose: the nominating process and the election of the next officers run on time, by the chapter's own rules, with a clean record — so the incoming officers are ready when their term starts.

> The process comes from your bylaws and from YPO's requirements: who nominates, who may vote, which majority, which deadlines, when the term starts. Read both; where they differ or are unclear, `[verify with Regional Manager]`. The body your bylaws name — often a Nominating Committee — leads.

## Who does what

| Yours | The Nominating Committee's or Chair's (as your bylaws name them) | Not yours |
|---|---|---|
| The timeline, built from the bylaws and the official notices | Asking candidates, building the slate, every conversation about people | Suggesting, ranking or commenting on candidates |
| Drafts: the call, the reminder, the voting notice, the announcement | Approving every text before it goes out | Counting a vote alone, or deciding a tie |
| The record: who was nominated (as the committee confirms it), the result, the date, the source | Confirming the result | Vote counts per person, unless the bylaws make them public |
| Starting the handover for every role that changes | | |

Ask once, early, who in your bylaws takes part — for example the Chair-Elect or the outgoing Chair — and who may nominate and vote. Not in the bylaws: `[verify with Regional Manager]`.

## Steps

1. **Find the rules.** Your assistant quotes the bylaws sections on nominations and elections — only text it can see in the file, with the section number (prompt 2). Missing: it says so.
2. **Build the timeline when the process opens.** Each step with a date and an owner (role). A date goes in only where the bylaws or a notice give it, or give a rule plus a date you supply; otherwise `[verify]`. YPO's own deadline for naming incoming officers belongs in your commitments calendar ([`../05-officer-handover/commitments-calendar.md`](../05-officer-handover/commitments-calendar.md)) `[verify with Regional Manager]`.
3. **Draft the messages.** Call for nominations, reminder, voting notice, announcement — each approved by the committee or the Chair, sent by you. If you set up an online ballot, the raw results go to the committee only — never into the assistant. Your assistant works only with the outcome the committee confirmed.
4. **Provisional until confirmed.** In `chapter-facts.md` the incoming officers stay `provisional` until the result is confirmed; then `confirmed`, with the date and the source (house rule 7).
5. **Record, report, hand over.** One line in `decisions.md` with the result and who confirmed it. Ask your Regional Manager how the new officers get into YPO's records and what YPO needs for their officer training `[verify with Regional Manager]`. Then start the officer briefings (prompt 9) and the handover checklist ([`../05-officer-handover/handover-checklist.md`](../05-officer-handover/handover-checklist.md)).

## Good to know

Discussions about candidates and reasons someone declined stay with the committee. The record says who was elected, when, under which rule.

## With your assistant

Prompt **36 Nominations timeline**: the bylaws quotes, the timeline and the message drafts — you check, the committee approves, you send.

## Your check

Every date traces to a bylaws sentence or a notice you can point at. No name is `confirmed` before the result is.
