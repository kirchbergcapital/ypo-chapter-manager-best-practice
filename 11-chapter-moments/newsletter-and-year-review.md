# Newsletter and year review

Purpose: a member newsletter or the review of the chapter year comes together in an hour instead of a weekend — written from the chapter's own files, approved by the Chair, and true in every number.

## Who does what

| Yours | The Chair's and officers' | Not for the newsletter |
|---|---|---|
| Collecting from the files you name: past events, upcoming dates, new members as announced, goals, thank-yous | The Chair's own words — a short note at the top | Anything a member has not agreed to see in print |
| A first draft, in the chapter's voice and length | Approving the whole text | Forum content, private news, numbers without a source |
| Photos only from events whose photo rules allow it | Choosing who gets a special thank-you | Member data copied from YPO Connect |

## A newsletter (monthly or quarterly)

1. **Name the period and the files.** For example: events since {{DATE}}, the event folders of the next three months, the welcomes already sent, the dates members need from `incoming-work.md`.
2. **Draft short.** Three to five items: what happened (one line each, a photo if allowed), what is next (date, place, link), one thank-you, one ask. A space for the Chair's note.
3. **Approve, then send.** The Chair approves; you send it from the chapter's mail tool — and a short post pointing to it, if that is your custom.

## The year review

1. **Collect the year.** Events with attendance from the check-in list or the debrief — if you only have RSVPs, it says "registered". Game plan goals as written in `game-plan.md`, with status and evidence (prompt 19). Officers and thank-yous from `chapter-facts.md`.
2. **Draft in three parts.** What we did · what we learned · what comes next. Every number names its file.
3. **Two versions.** The member version is warm and short. The internal version for the next ExCo also keeps the honest learnings — it goes into `07-reporting/` and the handover. Learnings never go into the member version unchanged.

For a design: the text goes into Canva ([`../07-connectors-and-browser/canva.md`](../07-connectors-and-browser/canva.md)).

## Good to know

Private news appears only if the member shared it for the newsletter. A number without a source becomes `[verify]`, not a guess.

## With your assistant

Prompt **37 Newsletter or year review draft** — you check, the Chair approves, you send.

## Your check

Every number names its file. Every person named has agreed or was already announced. "Registered" and "attended" are not mixed up.
