# Sad news and emergencies

Purpose: when a member, a spouse or partner, or someone close to the chapter dies or is seriously ill, the right people hear it from the right person, in the right order, and nothing automatic reaches the family. When an emergency hits an event, people are safe first and members get one clear, practical message.

These are two different moments with two different paths.

## A · Loss or serious illness

### Who does what

| Yours | The Chair's | Not yours to decide |
|---|---|---|
| Day one: pausing everything automatic that could reach the family or land badly | Whether, when and how the chapter is told; speaking with the family | What the family wants shared — the Chair asks, you follow |
| A draft message for the Chair, in the chapter's voice | Approving and signing the message | Details of illness or circumstances |
| Practical support: a card or flowers if the chapter does that, attendance at a service, a condolence address | Who represents the chapter; telling the Forum Officer | Anything about the person's Forum — the Forum Officer and the Forum handle that themselves |

### Day one

1. **Tell your Chair first.** Do not forward the news to anyone else.
2. **Pause what runs on its own** — until the Chair lifts the pause, not just for a week. Look in every tool, not only the folder:
   - scheduled mails and newsletters in your mail tool,
   - event registrations and reminders in the registration platform — the person's open registrations too,
   - birthday or anniversary automations,
   - scheduled WhatsApp or social posts,
   - open drafts in `06-communication/drafts/` (for example renewal reminders or the next newsletter).
3. **Ask for YPO's mail to pause.** Your Chair or you ask your Regional Manager how to stop YPO's own mail to the member — renewal notices, invitations — and what happens to the record `[verify with Regional Manager]`.
4. **The Forum hears it from its own people.** Your Chair tells the Forum Officer, who reaches the person's Forum. You do not message Forum members.

### The days after

5. **Wait for the family's words.** Your Chair, or whoever is closest, asks what may be shared. Until then nothing goes to members.
6. **Draft for the Chair.** The news exactly in the words the family allowed, one warm sentence, practical details (service, condolence address) only if confirmed. A private home address or phone number you add yourself in the final text, outside the assistant. Signed by the Chair.
7. **Send once.** The approved message goes out as agreed: one mail, and one post in the chapter group only if that is already your custom.
8. **The partner and the family.** If a spouse or partner is also a chapter contact, the Chair decides who writes to them. Partner and family stay on lists and invitations only as the family wishes.
9. **The quiet tasks, later.** A card or flowers if the chapter does that, a note in the chapter calendar so next year's mailings are thoughtful, and the record steps your Regional Manager gave you.

**Serious illness without a death:** a leave of absence or an attendance question goes to the Chair and the Regional Manager. The chapter shares only what the member allows.

## B · Emergency at an event — or a sudden cancellation

1. **Safety first.** At an accident or a medical emergency: emergency services, then the venue's staff. Then your Chair, or the deputy named in `chapter-facts.md`.
2. **Report it.** Your Chair or you tell your Regional Manager the same day; ask what YPO needs to know, for example for insurance `[verify with Regional Manager]`.
3. **One practical message the same day.** A venue cancelled, a speaker stuck, a change of place: what changes, what members do now, who to call. Your Chair approves it if it is more than logistics.
4. **Log it.** One line in the event's folder and in `00-handover/log.md`; learnings after the event.

## What stays out

Cause of death, diagnoses, family circumstances, condolence texts from members. Into the assistant goes only the wording the family allowed and the person's initials; it may look up the name in `chapter-facts.md` only to find drafts and registrations to pause. The name, and any private address, you add in the final text yourself. In the chapter folder: one log line with the date and what the chapter did.

Nothing in the draft that the family has not allowed. Every pause stays until the Chair lifts it. One sender, one send.
