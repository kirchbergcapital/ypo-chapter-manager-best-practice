# Lab B practice pack — one paste, one fictional email

Purpose: everything Lab B needs in one block — the prompt, a fictional email and the two bylaws sections it needs. Copy the block, paste it into your desktop task from Lab A (browser path: your project), and send that chat message. You do not send any email.

> **Fictional example — not a real chapter, not real rules.** Lakeside Chapter (fictional), from [`../examples/`](../examples/).

**You play Jamie, the fictional Chapter Manager of the Lakeside Chapter** — the draft will be in Jamie's voice.
Your assistant holds your own chapter facts, not Lakeside's — so expect [verify] marks in Jamie's draft; that is the assistant being honest, not failing.

## The block — copy all of it

```text
New email below, followed by our rules. 1) Say which folder it belongs in and give a file name with its extension (YYYY-MM-DD_sender-role_topic.md).
2) Give me one log line for 00-handover/log.md.
3) Draft a reply in my voice; answer only from the rules below and cite the section; mark every fact without a source as [verify]. I send it myself.
---
From:    Chris Example <chris@member-company.example>
To:      manager@lakeside-chapter.example
Date:    Wed, 14 Oct 2026, 22:41
Subject: Wine Dinner – still possible?

Hi Jamie,

sorry, I completely missed the deadline. Can I still sign up for the Wine Dinner on 22 October?
And could I bring my partner and one more guest?

Also – did we not agree last year that guests are free if they are family?

Thanks a lot,
Chris
---
Lakeside Chapter Bylaws (fictional), adopted by the ExCo on 2025-06-12 — excerpt.

Section 4.1 · Partners and guests at chapter events
Members may bring their partner to every chapter event unless the invitation says "members only".
Children and other guests may attend events marked "family" in the invitation. For any other event, a guest needs the
approval of the Learning Officer before the RSVP deadline. Guests pay their own share of the event cost.

Section 4.2 · RSVP deadline
Members reply by the RSVP deadline in the invitation, normally 10 days before the event.
After the deadline, the Chapter Manager may accept a registration only if the venue confirms there is capacity.
A registration after the deadline is confirmed in writing by the Chapter Manager; until then it is a request, not a seat.
```

## What a good answer looks like

- **Filed and logged:** a file name with its extension — `06-communication/inbox-filed/2026-10-14_member_wine-dinner-rsvp.md` — and one log line for `00-handover/log.md`.
- **Answered from the rules:** partner yes (Section 4.1); the other guest needs the Learning Officer's approval (4.1); a late registration only if the venue has capacity (4.2). No seat promised, "guests are free" not confirmed — and it flags that Section 4.1 does not say whether partners pay like guests `[verify]`.
- **A draft, not a send:** a friendly reply in Jamie's voice, for you to read. Nobody sends in the lab.

## Drafted or saved?

- **drafted** = the text is in the chat, not yet a file. Enough for a first run.
- **saved** = you can see the file in your folder: the email in `06-communication/inbox-filed/`, the line at the bottom of `00-handover/log.md`, the reply in `06-communication/drafts/`. Once your folder exists (Station 2): in the desktop app, type `Save all three now.` and the assistant saves them; in the browser, you save them.

Browser, no folder at hand? Ask your assistant: *"Create the Lakeside practice folder as one ZIP to download (examples + the ten empty folders)."*
