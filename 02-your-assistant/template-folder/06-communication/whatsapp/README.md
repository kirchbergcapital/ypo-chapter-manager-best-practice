# WhatsApp

Summaries that belong to no event — for example open conversations you hand over: who, topic, what is promised, next step. An event's summary goes into its event folder as `YYYY-MM-DD_whatsapp-summary.md`.
The exports themselves stay outside the chapter folder — on your phone, or in your own WhatsApp app's folder. A summary is a decision record: names only with a role or an RSVP, never next to a topic. The chat itself stays on your phone; every message that goes out is one you confirmed.
Forum groups and any chat with Forum content are never exported or summarised. Rule and details: `03-communication/whatsapp.md` in the kit.
