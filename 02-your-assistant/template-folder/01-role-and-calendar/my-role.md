# My role in our chapter — one page

Purpose: every new officer knows in two minutes what you do, what you don't do and how to reach you — without you explaining it again every July.
If your Chair or the region has a general Chapter Manager description, this page adds **your chapter's** view to it; where the two differ, talk it through with your Chair.
Agree it with your Chair once, then give it to every new officer. They can also ask their own AI: *"What can I ask our Chapter Manager, and what is mine?"*

**Fill it with your AI** — in your chapter folder, type:
```text
Help me write my one page "My role in our chapter" in 01-role-and-calendar/my-role.md.
Read my contract scope if it is in 01-role-and-calendar/, and chapter-facts.md. If I attach my areas.txt from my hours folder, use it too.
Ask me one question at a time, at most seven. Keep it to one page. Change the file only after I say yes.
```
