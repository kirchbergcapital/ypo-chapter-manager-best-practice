# 02 · Your assistant

> Set up an AI assistant that knows your chapter, follows your house rules, and keeps one folder true — so the chapter's memory stays with the role when officers change.

## What this is

Everything you need to go from "I have an AI account" to "my assistant files, drafts and remembers":
house rules (one file in the folder, plus a short core for your AI tool), a folder structure that belongs to the chapter, an interview that writes your chapter facts, and a lab that puts it all together.

## Why it matters

Chairs and officers rotate every year. You stay. Today much of the chapter's know-how sits in your head, your inbox and your phone.
This module moves it into one folder the chapter owns, so a stand-in or the next officer can continue from it. Your assistant reads that folder before it answers, drafts for you, and writes down what happened.
You send every message.

## How to use it with your AI

**1. First question after setup**

```
Read 02-chapter-knowledge/chapter-facts.md and 00-handover/handover.md.
Tell me in five lines what you know about our chapter and what is still marked [verify].
```

**2. When a rule question comes in**

```
An officer asks: {{QUESTION}}
Answer only from files in 02-chapter-knowledge. Quote file and section.
If no file covers it, write [verify with Regional Manager] and draft a short reply that says we are checking.
I send it myself.
```

**3. At the end of a working session**

```
Checkpoint now. Refresh 00-handover/handover.md: what is set up, open items
(each with what "done" looks like), paths touched. Then stop.
```

## Time needed

A planning guide, not a measured study. The bet: the hours come from searching and answering twice. Test it with your own week (log).

| Step | Time |
| --- | --- |

| Checkpoint at the end of a session | 2 minutes |
| Keeping `chapter-facts.md` current | When something changes, the same day |
