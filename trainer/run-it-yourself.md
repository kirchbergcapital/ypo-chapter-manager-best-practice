# Run it yourself

Purpose: how another trainer, a Regional Area Officer or an experienced Chapter Manager in YPO Europe can run this workshop for their own region — what you need, how to prepare, what you may change, what stays fixed, and how to send back what you learned.

The format was built to be passed on. If it helped Chapter Managers in one region, it should be easy to run in the next one.

> **Maintainer:** see [../README.md](../README.md) · **last checked:** 21 Oct 2026 · Menus move — every tool page in the kit carries an as-of date. Check those dates at T-14 and report what changed (see [Send back what you learned](#send-back-what-you-learned)).

## Who can run it

| You are … | Your role lens | Tip |
|---|---|---|
| A chair or past chair who also holds a regional role | Both lenses, as written in [format-guide.md](format-guide.md) | Read "Your two roles" there before anything else |
| A chair or past chair | Chair lens; replace the regional lens with patterns from the kit | Ask your Regional Manager for one regional observation you may share |
| A Regional Area Officer | Regional lens; ask a chair to give the chair view for 5 minutes in Block 5 | In your own region read [Running it in your own region](format-guide.md#running-it-in-your-own-region); as a guest elsewhere, [Running it as a guest](format-guide.md#running-it-as-a-guest) |
| An experienced Chapter Manager | `[Trainer: own example as Chapter Manager]` in every lens; a chair as guest for 5 minutes is a strong addition | Peers trust a peer — this may be the best version of the format |
| A professional AI trainer without a YPO role | Use the pattern lines from the kit; invite a chair or CM to give one example per block | Keep the one idea and the success test; they carry the session |

## What you need

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Your own practice | You have done Labs A, B and C yourself, in the desktop app with a demo folder, at least once |
| AI plan | A paid plan for your demo in the desktop app (the room sees what they will use). For participants there are two prerequisites — a paid plan, and chapter accounts in Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace for at least the CM and the Chair (`manager@`, `chair@`); the pre-workshop message says so |
| Laptop | Your own, with the desktop app, the demo folder, demo inbox, test chat export, the [mock portal](demo-assets/mock-portal/README.md), the 1-minute outlook video and `demo-backups/` |
| Demo accounts | A demo email inbox + a second test account with a fictional name; a fictional WhatsApp test group on your own number with one colleague (or the fictional export in [../examples/](../examples/)) — see [demo-scripts.md](demo-scripts.md) |
| The kit | This repo, unchanged or forked, with a QR code to the version you use |
| Time slot | 120 minutes (or 90 — see [format-guide.md § 90-minute version](format-guide.md#90-minute-version)) |
| Organiser | One person on the chapter or regional side who sends your messages to participants through the usual channel — before the day and after it ([follow-up.md](follow-up.md)). In a guest room, this is the host |

## Preparation timeline

### T-14 (two weeks before)

- [ ] Confirm date, room, length (120 or 90) and the expected number of participants with the organiser.
- [ ] Guest or own region? As a guest, agree with the host the handover sentence and that the anonymous harvest goes to the host ([format-guide.md § Running it as a guest](format-guide.md#running-it-as-a-guest)). In your own region, read [Running it in your own region](format-guide.md#running-it-in-your-own-region).
- [ ] Ask the organiser how participants are usually reached — and agree that **one** message goes out, through that channel, and that the follow-up after the day uses the same channel ([follow-up.md](follow-up.md)).
- [ ] Fill your role-lens placeholders in [format-guide.md](format-guide.md) (in your own copy). Delete any you cannot fill honestly.
- [ ] Set up or refresh your demo folder and task in the desktop app, demo inbox and test chat; allow folder access on T-1 ([demo-scripts.md](demo-scripts.md)).
- [ ] Decide moderation language ([format-guide.md § Language variants](format-guide.md#language-variants)).
- [ ] Check the kit's `[verify]` marks on tool facts (plans, connectors, settings) — tools change monthly. Note what you found for your feedback.

### T-7 (one week before)

- [ ] Organiser sends the pre-workshop message ([pre-workshop-message.md](pre-workshop-message.md)) with the link to [../00-before-the-workshop/](../00-before-the-workshop/README.md) and the short form. Plan and self-test status come back through the form, as counts — so nobody is asked in the room.
- [ ] Ask the venue: Wi-Fi for all participants at once, power at every seat, projector connection (bring adapters), tables for 3–4.
- [ ] Run all live demos once end to end. Record the backups (3–4 screenshots + one 30–45-second video each) and the 1-minute outlook video (your own WhatsApp app built with [../09-whatsapp-app-build-guide/](../09-whatsapp-app-build-guide/), browser agent on the mock portal, Canva — [demo-scripts.md § Outlook](demo-scripts.md#outlook--three-glimpses-in-one-minute)).
- [ ] Prepare the wall poster for the self-count (*running / homework*, handwritten on a flipchart) and the three feedback questions as a slide with a QR code to a short form ([harvest.md](harvest.md)). Agree with the organiser who sends the follow-up messages at T+2, T+14 and T+30 ([follow-up.md](follow-up.md)).
- [ ] Generate the QR code to the repo.

### T-1 (the day before)

- [ ] Organiser sends the short reminder ([pre-workshop-message.md](pre-workshop-message.md)).
- [ ] Rehearse every demo on the laptop you present from; check that connectors are still authorised.
- [ ] Browser path probe: in a fresh browser chat of ChatGPT and of Claude, attach the current `KIT-START.txt`, run Lab B with the Lakeside mail and save the draft; note the result for yourself.
- [ ] Download `_kit.zip` from the release link in a private browser window and unzip it once on Windows and once on a Mac: the folder must be `_kit` with `AI-GUIDE.md` inside.
- [ ] Copy `demo-backups/` and the fictional `.txt` files to a USB stick; set up a phone hotspot as Wi-Fi backup.
- [ ] Write the three questions by hand on a flipchart for the wall (or prepare the first slide); next to it an empty parking-lot sheet, *"That won't work for us — yet"*.
- [ ] Prepare the two "Chairs rotate" questions as one slide ([format-guide.md § Block 5](format-guide.md#block-5--persist-80106--question-3)).
- [ ] Re-read "Your two roles" in [format-guide.md](format-guide.md#your-two-roles--speaking-from-both-without-talking-down).

## The room

| Point | Set-up |
|---|---|
| Seating | Tables of 3–4, so neighbours can help each other; everyone can see the screen |
| Power | One socket per laptop — labs drain batteries |
| Wi-Fi | Tested with the venue for the full group; hotspot as backup |
| Wall | Three questions (KNOW, REACH, PERSIST) visible from minute 0; the parking-lot sheet next to them; the wall poster for the self-count |
| Your place | No lectern. You stand at the projector for demos and sit at a helpdesk table during the labs |
| Screen | Your demo laptop only; fictional data only |
| Helpers | With more than 15 people, one co-trainer or an experienced CM walks the room during the labs |
| Chairs | Movable — "Chairs rotate" needs people to change seats with their laptop |

## What you may adapt

| Adapt freely | Notes |
|---|---|
| Your role-lens examples | Your own, honestly; patterns from the kit if you have none |
| Moderation language | Material stays English; speak the room's language |
| The AI tool you demo with | The ChatGPT or Claude desktop app with one demo folder — the room should see files appear ([demo-scripts.md § The desktop set-up](demo-scripts.md#the-desktop-set-up--the-same-for-every-demo)); keep menu names out of your slides |
| The fictional chapter | Rename it, add local colour — keep it clearly fictional |
| Demo order *inside* a block | E.g. D2 before D1 if WhatsApp is the room's burning issue |
| 120 or 90 minutes | Use the 90-minute plan in the format guide |
| Extra time before or after | A chair talk, a CM round table, Q&A — before or after the 120 minutes, not inside them |
| Follow-up format | Online drop-in, a chat group run by the organiser, a second session |

## What stays fixed

| Fixed | Why |
|---|---|
| The one idea and the three questions | They make the session recognisable across regions |
| Order KNOW → REACH → PERSIST | The assistant built in Lab A is used in Labs B and C |
| Lab A whole (20 min) and the success test | The aim is that everyone leaves with something running — and knows what is homework |
| "Chairs rotate" in PERSIST | Someone else continues from your file — the moment that makes the one idea real |
| No public checks, no tally by the trainer | Plan and self-test status come from the pre-workshop form; the room counts itself with dots |
| House rules core: own files = truth, read before answering, draft never send, no guessing about people | They make the assistant trustworthy for chapter work |
| Fictional data on every screen | Nobody has to think twice about what they show or copy |
| A human sends — no bulk WhatsApp, no auto-send | The CM's number and name stay theirs |
| No claim to be official YPO material, no YPO logos | It is a community resource by members |
| Nothing from Forum or confidential sessions in the material or examples | Forum stays where it belongs |
| Anonymous harvest | Participants speak freely only if nothing goes back with a name |

## Send back what you learned

Every run makes the kit better. Within two weeks of your session (and again after the 30-day question), send the author (contact in [../README.md](../README.md)). The full cadence after the day is in [follow-up.md](follow-up.md).

| What | Format |
|---|---|
| Date, region (no chapter names), number of participants, 120 or 90 | One line |
| Success-test self-count: *running / homework* for 1, 2, 3, and *all three running* | From the wall poster in [harvest.md](harvest.md) |
| Answers to the three feedback questions | Summarised as themes |
| "What should go into the kit" | List, as written by participants |
| What you changed and why | Three lines are enough |
| Tool facts that were out of date (`[verify]` marks you checked) | File + line + what is true now |
| Which demo failed, and whether the fallback worked | One line per demo |
| 30-day question: which of the three steps still runs? | Counts and number of answers, after 30 days ([harvest.md](harvest.md#6--30-day-pulse--what-still-runs)) |

Proposed changes to the kit are welcome as a short note or, if you work with the repository directly, as a change request. Changes that touch "what stays fixed" are discussed with the author first.

### Share a prompt

The best prompts in the kit come from people who use them every week. If you or a participant has a prompt or template the kit is missing, send it to the maintainer with one line on what it is for — as a note or, if you work with the repository directly, as an issue. Take chapter facts out first: names, numbers, member details. Contributions are credited as "contributions from Chapter Managers"; names only if the person asks for it.
