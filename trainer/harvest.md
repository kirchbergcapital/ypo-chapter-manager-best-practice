# Harvest

Purpose: what you collect at the end of the day — the success self-count, three feedback questions and "what should go into the kit" — how it becomes an anonymous note for the region (or, in a guest room, for the host), and the 30-day question that shows what still runs.

Collected in Block 6 (show) and Block 7 (close) of the [format guide](format-guide.md). Takes no extra time if you prepare the wall poster. The full cadence after the day — who sends what, when, through which channel — is in [follow-up.md](follow-up.md).

## 1 · The show in pairs — self-count *running / homework*

During the show (min 106–112) everyone shows their partner, all at the same time; nobody presents to the room. Then (min 112–114) everyone counts themselves: three dots on the wall poster, one per row, and one more in the last row if all three run. **You do not count at the seats and you do not ask for hands.** You walk the show with nothing in your hand.

The wall poster — handwritten on a flipchart before the session:

| Result | Running | Homework |
|---|---|---|
| (1) Project answers from `chapter-facts.md` | | |
| (2) Folder structure in the cloud account | | |
| (3) Mail filed + log line + draft (not sent) | | |
| **All three running** | | — |

*Homework* is a good answer: the step is clear and the kit has it. What you want to know is the share that runs on the day and the share that goes home. After the session, count the dots and write the numbers into the note below.

**What you already know from before the day.** Paid plan set up, training setting checked, self-test done, WhatsApp as main channel — these came with the pre-workshop form ([pre-workshop-message.md](pre-workshop-message.md)), as counts. They are never asked in the room.

| From the pre-workshop form | Count |
|---|---|
| Paid AI plan set up | __ / __ answers |
| Training setting checked | __ / __ answers |
| Desktop app + folder test done | __ / __ answers |
| Browser path expected (no install, IT-managed laptop, iPad) | __ / __ answers |
| WhatsApp as main channel to members | __ / __ answers |

## 2 · Three feedback questions

A slide with a QR code to a short form, or sticky notes on the wall — whatever is fastest in the room. Anonymous.

1. **What will you use next week?** (one thing)
2. **What got in your way today?** (tool, time, account, anything)
3. **What would make your chapter easier to work for?** (one sentence — this is the one the region needs to hear)

## 3 · "What should go into the kit?"

One sticky-note wall or one extra form field: *"A template, prompt or example you wish this kit had."* Collect as written, together with the house-rule lines from the Lab A debrief and whatever stayed on the parking-lot sheet ([format-guide.md § Parking lot](format-guide.md#parking-lot-and-yes-and)). These are the best source for the next version — and they are how participants become co-authors of it.

## 4 · From the room to the region — anonymous

Within a week, write one short note. Send it to the author of the kit and, if agreed beforehand, to the Regional Manager or the region's working group on Chapter Manager topics. **In a guest room, the note goes to the host** — you said so at the close — and the host decides what goes further ([format-guide.md § Running it as a guest](format-guide.md#running-it-as-a-guest)).

**Rules for the note**

| Rule | How |
|---|---|
| No names | No participant names, no chapter names, no seat numbers |
| Counts and themes | "9 of 14 had all three steps running, 5 took one step home", "most frequent obstacle: paid plan not yet set up" |
| Quotes | Only paraphrased and only when nobody can be recognised; with fewer than 5 participants, themes only |
| Board issues | Report as a pattern ("several CMs receive requests from many officers in parallel"), never as a story about a chapter |
| What happens to the sheets | Count first, then destroy the wall poster and sticky notes after the note is written |

**Template**

```
Chapter Manager AI workshop — feedback for the region (guest room: for {{HOST}})
Date: {{DATE}} · Region: {{REGION}} · Participants: {{N}} · Format: 120 / 90 min

Success test, running / homework: (1) {{x1}}/{{x2}} · (2) {{y1}}/{{y2}} · (3) {{z1}}/{{z2}} · all three running {{all}} of {{N}}
Before the day (form): paid AI plan {{a}} · training setting {{t}} · desktop + folder test {{s}} · browser path {{p}} · WhatsApp main channel {{b}} · answers {{f}}/{{N}}

Will use next week (top 3 themes):
- …
Got in the way (top 3 themes):
- …
What would make chapters easier to work for (themes):
- …
Asked for in the kit:
- …

Suggested next step for the region (one line):
- …

30 days (added later): at least one step still runs {{p}} · nothing runs {{q}} · answers {{n}}/{{N}}
```

The third question is the bridge to the chairs: it tells the region, without names, what chapters could do for their Chapter Managers — often the [operating guardrails](../06-chapter-rules/operating-guardrails.md) in the participants' own words.

## 5 · Follow-up in two weeks

At the close you announced a follow-up. Use it to ask one question again: *"Which of the three steps still runs?"* Add the answer to your note to the author (see [run-it-yourself.md](run-it-yourself.md#send-back-what-you-learned)). Wording and timing: [follow-up.md](follow-up.md).

## 6 · 30-day pulse — what still runs?

Thirty days after the workshop, one anonymous question goes to all participants — through the organiser and the channel they already use, one message ([follow-up.md](follow-up.md), T+30):

> *"Which of the three steps still runs for you — the project with house rules, the chapter folder, the mail handoff, or none?"*

| Point | How |
|---|---|
| Anonymous | A short form without names, or answers collected by the organiser and passed on as counts only |
| One question | Nothing else — it takes a minute to answer. "Nothing runs" is the most useful answer, with a line on what got in the way |
| What you report | Counts per step, the share with at least one step still running, and **always the number of answers** — added to the note for the region (or the host) and sent to the author ([run-it-yourself.md](run-it-yourself.md#send-back-what-you-learned)) |
| Fewer than 5 answers | Report "too few answers" instead of counts |

This is the number that shows whether the format works beyond the day: not how many finished the lab, but how many still use a step a month later.
