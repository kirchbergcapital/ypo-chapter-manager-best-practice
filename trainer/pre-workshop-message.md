# Pre-workshop message

Purpose: the message participants receive seven days before the workshop, and a shorter reminder the day before. Ready to copy — fill the `{{PLACEHOLDERS}}`.

The T-7 message carries a short anonymous form (`{{FORM}}`). It does two jobs: it collects plan and self-test status **quietly, before the day** — so nobody is asked about it in the room — and it asks two questions that feed the workshop.

**Who sends it:** the organiser (for example the Regional Manager or the hosting chapter's CM), through the channel participants already use for this event. One message, one channel — not mail *and* WhatsApp *and* a calendar note.

## T-7 — one week before

**Subject (email):** Your AI workshop on {{DATE}} — 30 minutes to get ready

> Hello {{FIRST_NAME or "everyone"}},
>
> We are looking forward to seeing you on **{{DATE}}, {{TIME}}**, at **{{VENUE}}** for *Operational AI Excellence for Chapter Managers*.
>
> In two hours you set up your own AI assistant for chapter work: one folder for your chapter's knowledge, house rules for the assistant, and your first email handled from inbox to draft. We work in the **desktop app** of ChatGPT or Claude — whichever you already pay for — and the assistant saves every file into your folder itself. The aim: you leave with it running — and whatever is not finished on the day, the kit walks you through at home.
>
> **Please bring your own laptop** with the desktop app ready (see below) and its charger. Your phone is welcome too — chats and drafts work there on the go; building the folder happens on the laptop.
>
> **Two prerequisites:**
> 1. **A paid AI plan.** The kit is built for paid plans only, with training on your data switched off. If you don't have one yet, please set it up before the day — about 20 minutes extra, plus choosing the plan before you pay (a short checklist in the kit). The steps are in the link below.
> 2. **Chapter accounts in Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace** — at least one for you (`manager@`) and one for your Chair (`chair@`). They make the handover work and let your Chair read the chapter folder. If your chapter doesn't have them yet, ask your Chair this week — the ready request is in the kit; on the day you build in your own drive and move it across later.
>
> **Before the day — about 30 minutes:**
> 1. Laptop and account check (10 min)
> 2. The self-test (20 min): install the ChatGPT or Claude desktop app, create one folder `CM-Workspace` in your OneDrive or Google Drive, unzip the kit into it as `_kit` ({{KIT_ZIP_LINK}}), and let the assistant read one file there and save one itself. It also checks your training setting
>
> Both are here: **{{REPO_LINK}}/00-before-the-workshop/**
>
> **Then two minutes in this short form — anonymous:** {{FORM}}
> - Is your paid plan set up? Is the training setting checked? (yes / not yet — "not yet" is a fine answer; it only tells me how many lab neighbours to plan)
> - The desktop app: installed · not yet · my laptop is managed by IT. And the folder test — did the assistant save `done.txt`? (yes / not yet / didn't work)
> - Does your chapter have Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace accounts for you and your Chair (`manager@`, `chair@`)? (yes / only mine / not yet)
> - **Which question do you answer every year, for the third time, when a new officer starts?** Bring it — in the workshop it becomes the first question your assistant answers.
> - **One tool that disappeared again in your chapter — what was the reason?** (optional)
>
> If your paid plan or the desktop app is not ready on the day, or something doesn't work: come anyway. There is a browser path for every lab, you work next to a neighbour, and you set it up the same evening — everything you need is in the kit.
>
> See you on {{DATE}},
> {{TRAINER_NAME}}
>
> *This is a community workshop by YPO members, not an official YPO programme.*

## T-2 — when the message goes out late

Shorter: no form, no repo link, three steps. The folder is set up together in the room.

**Subject (email):** Your AI workshop on {{DAY}} — three short steps if you can

> Hello everyone,
>
> I am looking forward to seeing you on **{{DATE}}, {{TIME}}**, at **{{VENUE}}** for *Operational AI Excellence for Chapter Managers*.
>
> Our aim for the afternoon: a folder your AI assistant works in, and one chapter email taken from inbox to draft. Whatever is not finished on the day, the kit walks you through at home. Please bring your laptop, its charger, and the ChatGPT or Claude account you already pay for.
>
> If you have about 20 minutes before the day, three steps help the afternoon go smoothly:
>
> 1. **Training off, two-factor on.** ChatGPT: Settings → Data controls → switch off "Improve the model for everyone". Claude: Settings → Privacy → switch off "Help improve our AI models". On a Business or Team plan this is usually off already. While you are in the settings: switch on two-factor sign-in where your account offers it — for your email and cloud storage too.
> 2. **Desktop app.** Install the ChatGPT or Claude desktop app from the provider's website and sign in with your paid plan. (The ChatGPT Mac app needs macOS 14 or newer; otherwise the browser is fine.)
> 3. **One file.** On your laptop, open this link: {{KIT_LINK}} — it downloads `_kit.zip`. No account, nothing to install. If your browser asks, choose "Keep". Leave it in your Downloads folder; we unpack it together.
>
> If the plan, the app or the file is not ready on the day, come anyway. You will work next to a neighbour, and every exercise has a browser path.
>
> See you on {{DAY}},
> {{TRAINER_NAME}}
>
> *A community workshop by a YPO member, not an official YPO programme.*

`{{KIT_LINK}}` = https://github.com/kirchbergcapital/ypo-chapter-manager-best-practice/releases/latest/download/_kit.zip

## Notes for the sender

| Point | How |
|---|---|
| Personal or group | Personal one-to-one messages read better; a group mail is fine for email. On WhatsApp: one-to-one, sent by hand |
| Language | English by default; add the German line for German-speaking groups |
| Link | Point to the folder in the version of the kit you will use on the day; `{{KIT_ZIP_LINK}}` = the ZIP of that version, unzipped into `CM-Workspace/_kit` |
| The form | Three parts: status (paid plan, training setting, desktop app installed / not yet / IT-managed laptop, folder test passed, chapter accounts for CM and Chair — yes / not yet), the yearly officer question, the disappeared tool. Add "WhatsApp as your main channel to members? yes / no" if you want that count. Anonymous; the trainer sees counts and the questions, never who answered what |
| Why before the day | The status counts replace every show of hands in the room. The desktop and IT counts tell you how many will take the browser path. The trainer plans lab neighbours from them and never asks in public who is not ready ([format-guide.md § Block 2](format-guide.md#block-2--foundation-1020)) |
| The two questions | The yearly officer question feeds Lab A: participants write their first `chapter-facts.md` lines to answer it. The disappeared tool tells the trainer which objections to expect on the parking-lot sheet |
| Guest room | The host's organiser sends it; the form answers go to the trainer for planning and to the host with the harvest note |
