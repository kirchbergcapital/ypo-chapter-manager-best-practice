# Follow-up — after the day

Purpose: what happens in the 60 days after the workshop — who sends what and when, what you measure, how the kit moves from one version to the next, and two message drafts ready to fill.

The day itself ends with the show, the self-count and the feedback ([harvest.md](harvest.md)). This page starts where the room ends.

## One channel, agreed before the day

Every message after the workshop goes through **one channel agreed with the Regional Manager** (or the organiser who sent the pre-workshop message — in a guest room, the host). That is usually the way Chapter Managers already hear from their region.

| Do | Instead of |
|---|---|
| Agree the channel, the sender and the dates at T-14 ([run-it-yourself.md](run-it-yourself.md#t-14-two-weeks-before)) | Deciding after the day |
| One message per step, sent by the organiser or with their OK | A message from the trainer to everyone, one to many |
| The same channel for every step | Mail at T+2, WhatsApp at T+14, a form somewhere else at T+30 |
| Anonymous forms, results as counts | Asking people to reply to all |

## The cadence

| When | Who | What | Channel |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T+0** — in the room | Trainer | Show in pairs, self-count on the wall poster, three feedback questions, sticky-note wall "what should go into the kit" ([harvest.md](harvest.md)) | The room |
| **T+2** | Organiser (trainer drafts) | Thank you, **one** step for this week, link to an anonymous mini form (three questions) — draft below | The agreed channel |
| **T+7** | Trainer, internal | The anonymous note for the region — or, in a guest room, for the host — to the Regional Manager or the working group on Chapter Manager topics, if agreed beforehand ([harvest.md § 4](harvest.md#4--from-the-room-to-the-region--anonymous)). Count the wall poster and the form **before** the sheets are destroyed | Direct, one note |
| **T+14** | Organiser | One question: *"Which of the three steps still runs?"* — the announced follow-up, e.g. a 30-minute online drop-in | The agreed channel |
| **T+30** | Organiser (trainer drafts) | Same question + an invitation to send a template or prompt for the kit — draft below | The agreed channel |
| **T+60** | Trainer | Anonymous closing report to the Regional Manager and the working group: the three measures below, with n and limits | Direct, one report |

## Three measures

| # | Measure | How it is counted | Proposed threshold |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Ability in the room** | Share of participants with *all three running* on the wall poster at T+0 | at least two thirds |
| 2 | **Persistence** | Share of answers at T+30 with at least one step still running — **always reported together with the response rate** | at least half |
| 3 | **Transferability** | A second run is comparable after the changes made between runs, **and** at least one person other than the author has used [run-it-yourself.md](run-it-yourself.md) and sent feedback | both |

The thresholds are proposals, not promises — they are there so the closing report can say plainly whether the format did what it set out to do.

**How to state it.** Always with the base: *"In N runs, self-reported, n = …, response rate …"*. With fewer than 5 people in a run or fewer than 5 answers to a question: themes only, no rates.

## From v0.1 to v1.0

| Version | When | What changes | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **v0.1** | Before the first run | The kit as it is | — |
| **v0.2** | Between the first and the second run | Only learnings from the first run: the parking-lot sheet, the house-rule lines from the wall, "what should go into the kit", tool facts that were out of date | What stays fixed ([run-it-yourself.md § What stays fixed](run-it-yourself.md#what-stays-fixed)) does not change without the author |
| **v1.0** | After the second run **and** its T+30 answers | Learnings from both runs; the closing report's measures | Contributions from Chapter Managers credited as such; names only on request |

Each version gets one line in the kit's CHANGELOG: what changed and which run it came from — never who said it.
