# What stays yours — and what stays with the chapter

**In one sentence:** your know-how is yours; the chapter owns its memory. A clear line between the two protects you,
makes every handover easy, and lets you take your way of working to the next chapter.

**Self-employed or employed?** Chapter Managers work for their chapter — a small association, sometimes a small company — never for YPO Global.
If the chapter employs you, its rules, its AI rules included, and your contract set the details, such as who pays the AI plan and what you take with you `[check your contract]`.

## Why a clear line helps you

- **Your know-how is what you bring.** It grows with every event, every ExCo and every chapter. It belongs to you.
- **The chapter trusts a CM who keeps its memory in its own place.** Chapter data in the chapter folder is the
  strongest sign of a professional setup.
- **No awkward questions at the end.** When the line is clear from day one, the handover itself can be a 30-minute
  meeting.

## Yours

| What | Where it lives |
|---|---|
| Your know-how and experience — how events run, what works with officers, what to watch for | In your head and in your own notes |
| Your own templates and prompt library — general texts and prompts you brought or use with any chapter or client; for ones built for chapter work, agree what you take with you `[check your contract]` | Your own folder, outside the chapter folder |
| Your own method — how you plan the year, your checklists, your working rhythm | Your own folder |
| Your WhatsApp number and your chats | Your phone |
| A personal paid AI plan, if you have one, and the projects in it — a seat the chapter pays sits with the chapter's accounts | Your own account |
| Your own mail and devices; if self-employed, your invoices to the chapter | Your own accounts |
| Your hours log as a personal analysis — `hours.csv` and `areas.txt` ([`../10-time-by-area/`](../10-time-by-area/)); employed: next to the chapter's own working-time records | Your own folder, where the chapter's rules allow; the report is what you share, within those rules |

## The chapter's

| What | Where it lives |
|---|---|
| Chapter data — member and prospect notes, roster notes, finance | Chapter folder |
| Decisions, minutes, adopted rules | Chapter folder, `02-chapter-knowledge/` |
| Event files — contracts, venues, budgets, registrations | Chapter folder, `03-events/` |
| Role accounts (manager@, chair@), the chapter folder, the password manager | Owned by the chapter ([role-accounts.md](role-accounts.md)) |
| The chapter's AI seat on `manager@` and the chapter projects in it | The chapter's plan — access ends with the role |
| What the chapter needs from WhatsApp — decisions, RSVPs, promises | A short summary in the chapter folder; the chat stays on your phone |
| Texts written for this chapter — its invitation, its welcome mail | Chapter folder, `06-communication/templates/` |
| The handover note and the log | Chapter folder, `00-handover/` |

**A simple test:** would the next Chapter Manager need it to run this chapter? Then it goes into the chapter folder.
Would you use it with any chapter or client? Then it is yours. When you copy a chapter text into your own library,
take the chapter facts out first — names, numbers, member details — and keep the method.

## Keep the line, every day

- Chapter work in chapter accounts and the chapter folder; your library in your own folder. Two places, never mixed.
- Your assistant reads the chapter folder. Upload chapter files into your own AI project only as far as the work needs.
- WhatsApp: export → summary → file. What the chapter must keep lands in its folder within the week
  ([../03-communication/whatsapp.md](../03-communication/whatsapp.md)).
- Access ends with the role — yours and everyone else's. That protects you as much as the chapter: nobody can ask you
  about a mailbox you no longer hold.

## When your agreement ends — the handover

1. **Refresh the handover note** and write `00-handover/for-my-successor.md` — section B of
   [handover-checklist.md](handover-checklist.md) has the list and the prompt.
2. **Summarise open WhatsApp conversations** into the folder: who, topic, what is promised, next step.
3. **Tidy your own side:** chapter files you uploaded to your AI project or keep as local copies go back into the
   chapter folder or are deleted. Your templates and prompts stay with you.
4. **Close access:** the admins reset the role account passwords and remove you from the chapter folder and the
   password manager. You confirm in one line that you hold no chapter logins.
5. **Hold a 30-minute handover meeting** — you, the Chair and, if known, your successor. Walk through
   `00-handover/handover.md`, confirm the access list, agree what is still open. One line in `00-handover/log.md`.

**Prompt for your assistant:**

```
My agreement with the chapter ends on {{DATE}}. Read 00-handover/ and the chapter folder.
Draft three lists: (1) what the chapter needs from me before I leave, each with a date;
(2) chapter files that may also sit outside the folder (uploads in my AI project, local copies) for me to check;
(3) open WhatsApp conversations that need a summary in the folder.
Then draft a 30-minute agenda for the handover meeting with the Chair. Facts only from the folder; mark gaps UNCHECKED.
Draft only.
```

## Your setup is portable

Your way of working goes with you; the folder structure, the house rules and your prompt library too, as far as you agreed with the chapter `[check your contract]`.
Your next chapter or client benefits too: you start on day one with a system that already runs — filled with their
facts instead of the old ones. That is what a professional brings to the table.

---
*A practical guide, not legal advice. Where your agreement with the chapter says something specific, the agreement
