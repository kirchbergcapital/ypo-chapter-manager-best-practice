# Commitments calendar — your template for the year

> **This template is empty on purpose.** Copy the current Chapter Commitments — names, definitions and deadlines —
> from the official Chapter Commitments page in the Chapter Management Hub (CMH), or ask your Regional Manager for them.
> Definitions and dates change; the official source is always the current one.
> Which commitments involve you is something you agree with your Chair.

Copy this file to `01-role-and-calendar/commitments-calendar.md`, fill it once at the start of the year, and review it
once a month with your assistant.

## Fill it with your assistant

Paste the current text from the official source below the line:

```
Fill 01-role-and-calendar/commitments-calendar.md from the official Chapter Commitments text I paste below.
One row per commitment: official wording, deadline, file in our folder. Leave "CM's part" as a question to me
where it is not clear. Do not add commitments, numbers or dates that are not in the pasted text.
Mark every gap [verify]. Draft only.
---
{{PASTE_FROM_CMH}}
```

## Monthly check with your assistant

```
Monthly commitments check. Read 01-role-and-calendar/commitments-calendar.md and the files it points to.
For each commitment: status (met / on track / at risk / UNCHECKED), the file that shows it, and the next date.
List what is due in the next 60 days and who owns it. Then draft a five-line status note for the Chair.
Facts only from the folder; mark everything else [verify]. Draft only.
```

Add one line to `00-handover/log.md` after each check. When the official definitions change, copy them again and note
the date in the "Source" line.
