# Glossary

Purpose: every abbreviation and term this kit uses, in one place. **All entries describe the situation as of October 2026.**
Terms marked `[verify]` may differ in your chapter or region — your Regional Manager has the current wording.

## Roles

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **CM** | Chapter Manager — runs the chapter's day-to-day work: events, members, finance, ExCo support. Every CM works for their chapter, self-employed or employed — never by YPO Global; questions about the contract itself go to your Chair or your Regional Manager. Often the person who stays longest |
| **Chair** | Chapter Chair — leads the chapter and its ExCo for one chapter year. For the exact dates of the year, ask your Regional Manager |
| **Chair Elect** | The member chosen to become Chair at the start of the next chapter year |
| **ExCo** | The chapter's executive committee (board): the Chair and the officers |
| **Officer** | A member who holds a chapter role for the year, e.g. Learning, Forum or Membership Officer. The core officer roles are set by YPO; each chapter can add more `[verify]` |
| **Learning Officer** | The officer who owns the chapter's events and learning calendar — usually the officer a CM works with most |
| **RAO** | Regional Area Officer — a volunteer regional officer who supports a group of chapters; the term length: ask your Regional Manager |
| **Regional Manager** | YPO staff member for a region; for most CMs the main contact for questions about YPO rules, data and dates `[verify]` |

## YPO terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Forum** | A small, confidential peer group of members — the core of YPO. The kit's rule: Forum is sacred. We use AI for Forum logistics only: dates, places, headcounts, costs, deadlines. Never for what is said in or around a Forum: topics, stories, notes, retreat conversations, anything shared in confidence, or any link between a name and a topic. If in doubt, leave it out |
| **Game Plan** | The chapter's plan for the year: goals with owners and metrics, agreed by the chapter at a facilitated session (Game Day). The chapter commits to the goals, the Chair owns delivery, the CM keeps the file current — see [`05-officer-handover/game-plan-keeping.md`](05-officer-handover/game-plan-keeping.md). Format and timing in your region: ask your Regional Manager `[verify]` |
| **RBM** | Regional Board Meeting — where regional and chapter leaders meet; how often your region holds one: ask your Regional Manager |
| **Chapter Commitments** | YPO's minimum standards every chapter makes to its members. The current definitions and deadlines are on the official Chapter Commitments page in CMH — or ask your Regional Manager. The kit has an empty calendar to copy them into: [`05-officer-handover/commitments-calendar.md`](05-officer-handover/commitments-calendar.md) |
| **CMH** | Chapter Management Hub — YPO's online tool where chapters maintain officers, forum data and events, with dashboards `[verify]` |
| **YPO Gold** | The part of YPO for members after their years in YPO, with its own Gold chapters. Who moves, when and how is set by YPO — your Regional Manager has the current rules. The kit's page: [`11-chapter-moments/moving-to-gold.md`](11-chapter-moments/moving-to-gold.md) |
| **YPO Connect** | YPO's member platform and directory, in the browser |
| **Cvent** | The event registration platform YPO uses; chapter events can be published there `[verify for your region]` |

## AI and tools

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Project (AI)** | A workspace in your AI tool with its own instructions and files, so every chat starts with your house rules. Names differ by tool `[verify]` |
| **Connector** | A link that lets your AI read from (or write drafts to) another service, such as your mailbox, your cloud storage or Canva. What is available depends on tool and plan (as of Oct 2026, `[verify]`) |
| **Training opt-out** | The setting that stops your AI provider from using your chats to train its models. Where it sits differs by tool — see [`01-foundations/paid-plan-and-training-off.md`](01-foundations/paid-plan-and-training-off.md) |
| **Role account** | An email address and login that belong to the role, not the person — e.g. `manager@` or `chair@` at the chapter's domain, in the chapter's Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace. It passes to the next person with everything in it. Required by this kit: at least `manager@` for the Chapter Manager and `chair@` for the Chair. Your WhatsApp number is not a role account — it stays yours |
| **Knowledge base** | The chapter's folder of short, dated files that your AI answers from: rules, event playbooks, contacts by role, decisions |
| **Markdown** | Plain text with a few symbols for headings, lists and tables (`#`, `-`, `|`). Every AI reads it well; it opens on any computer and never locks you in. |
| **Claude Code** | Anthropic's AI agent that works directly with files and folders on your computer. Used at Level 3 only |
| **Paid plan** | A paid subscription to an AI tool (e.g. Claude Pro, ChatGPT Plus). One of the kit's two prerequisites (the other: chapter accounts `manager@` and `chair@`); free plans are not part of it |
| **Baileys** | An open-source, unofficial library that connects to WhatsApp the way WhatsApp Web does. The small app you can build with [`09-whatsapp-app-build-guide`](09-whatsapp-app-build-guide/) uses it to read chats and draft replies — sending is optional, one confirmed message at a time |

## Kit words

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Truth · Source** | Your own files are the truth. Emails, chats, PDFs and exports are sources: you cite them and update the truth |
| **UNCHECKED** | The assistant could not open the file or did not look. Never the same as "fine" |
| **`[verify]`** | A fact nobody has checked yet. Check it before it goes to anyone |
| **Checkpoint** | A short refresh of `00-handover/handover.md`: what is set up, what is open, which files changed |
| **Level 1 · 2 · 3** | How deeply you set up your AI: chat project · living folder · Claude Code in your folder (a repository is optional) — see [`01-foundations/three-levels.md`](01-foundations/three-levels.md) |
| **Hours line** | One line per finished task in a fixed form: `HOURS · YYYY-MM-DD · <area> · <event folder or -> · <task in a few words, roles not names> · <minutes> min · suggested\|confirmed`. Your assistant suggests the minutes; only lines you confirm count. They go into `hours.csv` in your own folder — see [`10-time-by-area/`](10-time-by-area/) |
| **Area** | One kind of work your hours are counted under, such as "Member communication", "Learning and chapter events" or "Forum logistics". About ten, in your own words, one per line in `areas.txt` in your own folder — see [`10-time-by-area/areas-template.txt`](10-time-by-area/areas-template.txt) |
| **Incoming work list** | One open list in `01-role-and-calendar/incoming-work.md` of trainings, surveys, reports, forms, meetings and other asks still open, so you and a stand-in see what is still open (prompt 29) |
| **Incoming work sweep** | Once a week your assistant reads the chapter mail (reading only) and the chats you choose and offers rows for the incoming work list (prompt 40) |
| **Folder map** | `folder-map.md` in a chapter folder with its own structure: the chapter's folder for each kit folder, so nothing is copied or moved |
| **Platform field map** | The file `02-chapter-knowledge/platform-fields.md` that points each screen field at the chapter file which already holds the value, so you type a fact once and copy it (prompt 30) |
